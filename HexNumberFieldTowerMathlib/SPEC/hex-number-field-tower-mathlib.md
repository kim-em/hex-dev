# hex-number-field-tower-mathlib (depends on hex-number-field-tower + hex-number-field-mathlib + hex-resultant-mathlib + hex-berlekamp-zassenhaus-mathlib + hex-row-reduce-mathlib)

## Correspondence-only classification

This library is a `correspondence-only-layer`.

Computational conformance owners: `HexNumberFieldTower`, `HexNumberField`
Computational performance owners: `HexNumberFieldTower`, `HexNumberField`

Mathlib companion for `hex-number-field-tower`. It interprets every validated
tower as a finite extension of `ℚ` with a fixed embedding into `ℂ`, proves the
coordinate field operations, and verifies Trager factorization, adjoining,
splitting fields, and primitive-element flattening.

The total forms in `## Total forms` are `Option.get` applied to the
`Option`-valued operations with their completeness proofs. They add no
computation, so the classification stands and the `Option`-valued operations
remain the conformance and performance owners.

## Semantic tower

Associate to each `T : NumberTower` a fixed injective complex interpretation.
The initial semantic boundary is a plain function so its proofs do not
presuppose the field structure they are intended to justify.

```lean
noncomputable def NumberTower.eval? (T : NumberTower) (a : T.Elem) :
    Option ℂ
noncomputable def NumberTower.toComplex (T : NumberTower) (a : T.Elem) : ℂ

theorem NumberTower.toComplex_injective (T : NumberTower) :
    Function.Injective T.toComplex
```

After arithmetic correspondence is proved, install the law-bearing field
structure on `NumberTower.Elem T`, package `toComplex` as a ring embedding, and
prove `dim T = finrank ℚ T.toField` (where `toField` abbreviates `Elem T`
carrying the scoped field structure). This order keeps the public
theorems independent of whether the proof is organized through iterated
`AdjoinRoot` or intermediate fields generated by the stored complex roots.

## Level invariant

For every validated level, prove:

1. the defining polynomial maps to an irreducible polynomial over the semantic
   lower field;
2. evaluating it at the stored absolute generator through the lower embedding
   gives zero;
3. the coordinate quotient is equivalent to adjoining that exact generator;
4. the resulting complex map is injective.

This is the central invariant. Irreducibility proves the quotient is a field;
the zero evaluation proves that its chosen embedding is the intended conjugate.
The private-constructor policy means induction over `rat`, `ofPolyQuot`, and
successful `adjoin?`/`split?` results suffices.

## Arithmetic correspondence

```lean
theorem NumberTower.map_add (T) (a b : T.Elem) :
    T.toComplex (a + b) = T.toComplex a + T.toComplex b
theorem NumberTower.map_mul (T) (a b : T.Elem) :
    T.toComplex (a * b) = T.toComplex a * T.toComplex b
theorem NumberTower.map_inv (T) (a : T.Elem) :
    T.toComplex a⁻¹ = (T.toComplex a)⁻¹
```

Also prove coordinate uniqueness, `dim T = finrank ℚ T.toField`, install the
`Lean.Grind.Field (NumberTower.Elem T)` law package for the computational
operations (delegated to Mathlib's standard `Field.toGrindField` bridge in
the same scope as the field instance; the substantive content is the scoped
`Field`/`CharZero` pair), and prove that every checked-constructor
`Extension.embed`, i.e. one satisfying `Extension.PreservesEmbedding`, is an
injective `ℚ`-algebra homomorphism under its semantic interpretation.

## Trager factorization

```lean
theorem NumberTower.factor?_sound {T} (f : Poly T) {r}
    (h : factor? f = some r) :
    Factorization.Sound T f r

theorem NumberTower.factor?_isSome {T} (f : Poly T) :
    (factor? f).isSome
```

`Factorization.Sound` states reconstruction including the scalar, monicity,
positive multiplicities, irreducibility over `T.toField`, no associates, and
deterministic ordering. The no-associates clause is not a separate conjunct:
monicity together with the strict `factorsSorted` ordering already forbids
associated factors, since associated monic polynomials are equal and the
strict order rules out duplicates.

The proof is organized by Yun squarefree component:

1. Transfer Yun decomposition and its multiplicity index through
   `equivField`.
2. At a level `K(α)/K`, identify the one-level executable resultant with the
   relative field norm by full resultant agreement and the root-product formula.
3. For `d = [K(α):K]` and `m = degree g`, prove that among the first
   `choose(d*m, 2) + 1` signed integer shifts, one relative norm is squarefree
   by counting collisions of its `d*m` conjugate roots.
4. Recursively factor that relative norm over `K`, reaching integer
   factorization only at the rational base tower.
5. Prove relative gcd recovery produces exactly the irreducible factors over
   `K(α)` and that the checked reconstruction predicate succeeds.
6. Reassemble the Yun components, preserving their multiplicities.

The induction is necessarily level-by-level. An absolute norm is not a valid
substitute: for example, in `ℚ(√2, √3)`, a factor defined over `ℚ(√3)` is
duplicated by the unused `√2` embeddings, so every absolute norm after shifting
only by `√3` has repeated factors.

The vanishing criterion of `hex-resultant-mathlib`
(`resultant_eq_zero_iff_common_root`) is insufficient for steps 2 and 5; they
rest on its full value correspondence
(`resultant_eq_leadingCoeff_mul_prod_roots`).

## Adjoining

```lean
theorem NumberTower.adjoin?_sound (T) (a : AlgebraicRoot) {E}
    (h : T.adjoin? a = some E) :
    Extension.Sound T a E

theorem NumberTower.adjoin?_isSome (T) (a : AlgebraicRoot) :
    (T.adjoin? a).isSome
```

`Extension.Sound` states that `embed` preserves the old embedding, `gen` maps
to `a.toComplex`, the new tower is generated by the image and `gen`, and an
identity extension is returned exactly when `a.toComplex` already lies in the
embedded old tower.

Soundness follows from factor correctness and candidate evaluation.
Completeness of factor selection uses `evalDisambiguationPrec` and its
evaluation-eliminant lower bound for every nonvanishing factor.

## Splitting fields

```lean
theorem NumberTower.split?_sound {T} (f : Poly T) {S}
    (h : split? f = some S) :
    Splitting.Sound T f S

theorem NumberTower.split?_isSome {T} (f : Poly T) :
    (split? f).isSome
```

`Splitting.Sound` states that the inclusion preserves coefficients, the mapped
polynomial is reconstructed by the returned linear roots with the listed
multiplicities, and the extension is generated over `T` by those roots. The zero
polynomial is represented by `NumberTower.Roots.all` rather than a finite root
array.

Termination uses the sum of degrees of nonlinear factors after each genuine
adjoining step. Completeness uses algebraic closedness of `ℂ`, absolute
eliminant completeness, and `adjoin?_isSome`.

## Flattening

```lean
theorem NumberTower.flatten?_sound (T) {F}
    (h : T.flatten? = some F) :
    F.Sound

theorem NumberTower.flatten?_isSome (T) :
    T.flatten?.isSome

theorem NumberTower.flatten_toComplex (T) {F}
    (h : T.flatten? = some F) (a : T.Elem) :
    PolyQuot.toComplex (F.toPrimitive a) F.root.rep F.root.rep_mk =
      T.toComplex a
```

The direct full-degree search scans with validated linear-gcd recovery only.
Its total fallback retains the shift of the bounded maximum-degree candidate
from `AlgebraicPoly.Common.extendShift?`; primitive-element theory proves that
this candidate generates the two-input compositum. The fallback first tries
the same validated gcd and otherwise uses exact trace pairing in that
proved-equal field. Row-reduction correspondence proves the recovered
coordinates. The checked tower-basis round trip extends to all elements by
linearity; the primitive polynomial relation, irreducibility, and equal
dimensions yield the opposite round trip and multiplicativity.
`AlgebraicRoot.exact_toComplex` identifies the canonical primitive root stored
in the result.

## Total forms

```lean
def NumberTower.adjoin (T) (a : AlgebraicRoot) : Extension T
def NumberTower.factor {T} (f : Poly T) : Factorization T f
def NumberTower.split {T} (f : Poly T) : Splitting T f
def NumberTower.flatten (T) : Flattening T

@[simp] theorem NumberTower.adjoin?_eq_some (T) (a) :
    T.adjoin? a = some (T.adjoin a)
@[simp] theorem NumberTower.factor?_eq_some {T} (f) :
    factor? f = some (factor f)
@[simp] theorem NumberTower.split?_eq_some {T} (f) :
    split? f = some (split f)
@[simp] theorem NumberTower.flatten?_eq_some (T) :
    T.flatten? = some T.flatten
```

Each total form is `Option.get` of the `Option`-valued operation with its
completeness theorem, so `adjoin T a` is definitionally the extension
`adjoin? T a` returns. The rewriting lemmas transport every soundness
statement about the `Option`-valued form to the total one.

## Developments

1. Iterated quotient-field semantics and fixed complex embeddings.
2. Coordinate basis, dimension, and arithmetic correspondence.
3. Resultant-as-norm for each tower level and recursively over the tower.
4. Characteristic-zero shift bounds and Trager gcd recovery.
5. Adjoin factor-selection soundness and embedding preservation.
6. Splitting termination, reconstruction, and generated-field minimality.
7. Primitive-element degree test and flattening coordinate equivalences.

Items 3 through 7 rest on that resultant value correspondence.

## External comparators

No external comparator is required.

**Justification:** `correspondence-only-layer` per
`SPEC/benchmarking.md §"Comparator naming"`. The library introduces no
tower arithmetic or factorization algorithm; it verifies the executable
tower operations and the Trager pipeline implemented elsewhere. The
computational performance owners are hex-number-field-tower, where the
dimension ladders and the PARI/GP nffactor comparator are measured, and
hex-number-field for the base-level arithmetic it transports.
