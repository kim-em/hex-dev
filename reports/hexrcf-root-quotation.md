# Ordinary quotation of supplied root replay

`RootQuote.quote` reconstructs the value of an `Except String` reader together
with a checked equation `Except.toOption reader = some value`. It composes the
owners' public replay equations with ordinary congruence and conditional laws.
It never treats a compiled reader result as proof evidence. A scalar callback
may propose an equation for a demanded decode; the exact equation, ordinary
axiom dependencies and kernel term are checked before use.

Public graph and node equations expose literal replay boundaries. The normalizer
preserves those heads, follows the demanded recursor/projection path and uses
normal cached reduction for closed rational arithmetic. Every temporary
reducibility change is confined by restoring the entire environment around that
normalization. Dependent Boolean decisions are discharged as complete
conditionals: changing a Boolean major alone would leave a sibling proof tied
to the old major. The implementation instead checks the Boolean equality,
derives the condition or its negation and applies the core conditional law.

The helper continues the caller's shared reflection budget. Traversal and
normalization boundaries consume proof nodes; constructed proofs are charged
before checking. No separate replay fuel is reset at recursive boundaries.
Caller heartbeat, recursion and cancellation limits remain effective. Successful
calls restore caller metavariables and retain checked private auxiliaries plus
the environment effects of accepted callbacks;
rejection, structured exhaustion and exceptions restore the complete saved
state. Rejected input, including parsing or checking failure, is terminal
invalid-provider evidence, not an unsupported coefficient decline.

The independent cubic fixture supplies `X³−3`, open endpoints −8 and 8 and a
complete finite replay graph. Its supplied subject and evidence use frozen
constructors, including rational payload arrays
`[0,n,1]`. The finite scalar callback uses the public `NumberField.decode_payload`
equation delivered by merged PR #10970; each proposal is checked at the exact
demanded expression. A separate strict constructor-closure audit excludes
producer calls and raw recursors from the supplied JSON. A computed-JSON
negative control must fail that audit. A public fresh-module consumer proves
the accepted reader retains the original context, polynomial, endpoints and selection
metadata using the owner's subject-preservation theorem. It constructs the
actual rational model, interprets the original cubic and proves an ordinary
real witness `x³ = 3 ∧ −8 < x ∧ x < 8` from the accepted descriptor. No supplied
root or infinitesimal witness is assumed. A non-module audit
checks the actual theorem bodies and their complete transitive axiom closure,
requiring exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. It also verifies
that actual public codec, replay and core conditional laws occur in the proof
closure and that temporary reducibility metadata has not escaped. Generated
quotation bodies must directly use the asserted graph, node, moment, table,
Array traversal, core conditional and subsingleton laws. The cached-checking
rule is present transitively but does not occur directly in these generated
bodies; these fixtures do not exercise that rule. This is not exhaustive coverage of
every possible expression or alternative witness form.

Regressions test continued aggregate usage, exact proof limits and limits one
node below success, early and late exhaustion, malformed graph versions and
mathematically false counts. The changed-count node parses successfully before
its replay is rejected. Callback tests mutate declarations and metavariables
before declining, proposing a proof of another equation, failing or interrupting.
A well-typed false equation is wrapped in an expected-type hint around a
reflexivity proof: Meta type inference agrees, but the kernel must reject it.
An untrusted inadmissible candidate for the correct equation is rejected before
any admitted declaration can be installed. Caller-disabled kernel checking is
overridden. Both polarities of dependent conditionals and a non-Boolean
recursor returning a function have positive controls. Accepted callback effects
follow the same success-state policy as the dispatcher.
Independent EIO observers cannot restore state on behalf of the production API.
Lowered heartbeat/recursion limits and cancellation remain terminal; an unchanged
reader still succeeds after all failures.

This does not register a general tactic handler, authenticate provider or
predecessor histories or prove complete root coverage. The concrete rational
model above does not construct a model for arbitrary provider/predecessor
histories. A reader equation composes with those separate source and semantic
proofs. Generic scalar recognition, strict compiled missing-record rejection,
general original-source quotation, realization and the remaining performance
and completeness evidence remain obligations of #10358.

reader or arithmetic implementation.

These are shared-host build observations with dependency work and differing
scopes, not matched performance comparisons or general degree/depth scaling.
Compiled artifact sizes include separate public/private/server files where
present and do not measure expanded proof work. Existing #10633/#10634 evidence
and its qualified conclusions remain unchanged. Local Lean/Lake/render work
stays serial under a hard aggregate 32 GiB cap, no swap and two Lean threads.

Each rewrite motive embeds the current reader. Repeated candidate accounting
and auxiliary statements can therefore grow with the number of steps times the
term size; normalization also reopens its scoped cache at successive cuts.
The cubic's 191,702 charged nodes use about 19% of the default million-node
reflection budget. This explicitly accounts for expansion and is not a physical
sharing or scalability claim. Quotation regressions use 12,000,000 Lean
heartbeats and recursion depth 8192; the fresh body audit uses depth 32768.
Success under Lean's default options has not been established. These limits
remain caller-controlled and are not reset by the helper.
