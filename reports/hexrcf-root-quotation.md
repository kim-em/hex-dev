# Ordinary quotation of supplied root replay

`RootQuote.quote` reconstructs the value of an `Except String` reader together
with a checked equation `Except.toOption reader = some value`. It composes the
owners' public replay equations with ordinary congruence and conditional laws.
It never treats a compiled reader result as proof evidence. A scalar callback
may propose an equation for a demanded decode; the exact equation, ordinary
axiom dependencies and kernel term are checked before use.

Public graph and node equations expose literal replay boundaries. The normalizer
preserves those heads, follows the demanded recursor/projection path and uses
normal cached reduction for closed rational arithmetic. Every temporary
reducibility change is confined by restoring the entire environment around that
normalization. Dependent Boolean decisions are discharged as complete
conditionals: changing a Boolean major alone would leave a sibling proof tied
to the old major. The implementation instead checks the Boolean equality,
derives the condition or its negation and applies the core conditional law.

The helper continues the caller's shared reflection budget. Traversal and
normalization boundaries consume proof nodes; constructed proofs are charged
before checking. No separate replay fuel is reset at recursive boundaries.
Caller heartbeat, recursion and cancellation limits remain effective. Successful
calls retain checked private auxiliaries and restore caller metavariables;
rejection, structured exhaustion and exceptions restore the complete saved
state. Invalid evidence is terminal invalid-provider evidence, not an unsupported
coefficient decline.

The independent cubic fixture supplies `X³−3`, open endpoints −8 and 8 and a
complete finite replay graph. All its JSON fields are frozen constructors, including rational payload arrays
`[0,n,1]`. The finite scalar callback uses the public `NumberField.decode_payload`
equation delivered by merged PR #10970; each proposal is checked at the exact
demanded expression. A separate strict constructor-closure audit excludes
producer calls from the supplied JSON. A public fresh-module consumer proves the accepted
reader retains the original context, polynomial, endpoints and selection
metadata using the owner's subject-preservation theorem. A non-module audit
checks the actual theorem bodies and their complete transitive axiom closure,
requiring exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. It also verifies
that actual public codec, replay and core conditional laws occur in the proof
closure and that temporary reducibility metadata has not escaped.

Regressions test continued aggregate usage, exact proof limits and limits one
node below success, early and late exhaustion, malformed graph versions and
mathematically false counts. The changed-count node parses successfully before
its replay is rejected. Callback tests mutate declarations and metavariables
before declining, proposing a proof of another equation, failing or interrupting.
Independent EIO observers cannot restore state on behalf of the production API.
Lowered heartbeat/recursion limits and cancellation remain terminal; an unchanged
reader still succeeds after all failures.

This does not register a general tactic handler, authenticate provider or
predecessor histories, prove complete root coverage or discharge a real-model
obligation. A reader equation composes with those separate source and semantic
proofs. Generic scalar recognition, strict compiled missing-record rejection,
general original-source quotation, realization and the remaining performance
and completeness evidence remain obligations of #10358.
