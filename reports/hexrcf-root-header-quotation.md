# Binding a quoted root to its original subject

`RootHeader.quoteWith` reconstructs the result of the original supplied
`RootReplay.readDescriptor` application and independently parses its raw
subject. It derives the root/header equality through the existing owner's
`readDescriptor_subject` theorem. This removes a handwritten source-identity
proof from subsequent scalar-packet quotation.

The helper takes the full original application. It retains its explicit and
implicit arguments when instantiating the owner law and takes the subject
reader from that instantiated law. This avoids substituting newly synthesized
operation instances while comparing dependent types. Both reader quotations,
the strict equations and the final checked binding consume the caller's
reflection budget. The enclosing `RootQuote.runWith` supplies the transaction.
The helper does not construct a source expression's algebraic presentation or
an origin/provider history.

The public example starts from the frozen cubic reader. It exports a newly
reconstructed root and raw subject, constructs a context using that root,
quotes the original sign packet for X, and decodes a coefficient using the
new fact. Ordinary-real interpretation identifies the coefficient with that
new root and proves positivity. The new packet/value closure rejects any
dependency on the old fixture's `root_raw`, `subject_read` or
`nativeRoot_read` identities.

Default quotation recognizes the actual canonical signature roundtrip,
using `Signature.codec_lawful` at the encoded signature argument. Two distinct
signature subjects are tested; a proof for one cannot prove the other.
A fixture-specific signature decoder is unnecessary for these expressions.
`SignatureQuote.decoder` additionally consumes the owner's public
`Signature.codec_literal` equation and a checked full-literal binding. The
cubic packet retains frozen numerical node and nested-moment signatures for
its rational predecessor. Its exported evidence is required to contain only
JSON constructors, integer constructors and raw natural literals. These
serialization identities do not validate arbitrary registries, selected roots
or origin histories, or freeze deeper algebraic frames.

The importing audit rechecks 710 theorem bodies and 66 definitions in its
explicitly declared source scope, with 761 nonempty diagnostic traces. Its
axiom closure is transitive and restricted to `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound`. The native-producer exclusion guard is unchanged. The
scope includes the generated root/header bindings, packet acceptance and
coefficient/value proofs, together with their reached private bodies; it is
not an assertion that every theorem in the repository was audited.

The root/header and subsequent packet requests are checked separately in the
fixture. These counts include repeated expression occurrences; they do not
measure unique DAG nodes, physical sharing or a general speedup. The helper's
`quoteWith` form can compose within a caller's existing request.

Header controls check exact and one-node-below budgets, rejection of a partial
reader application, malformed versions and an invalid root count. Invalid-count
replay must exercise the callback before the test checks full caller-state
restoration. Packet controls independently check compiled rejection, ordinary
quotation refusal and caller restoration for a wrong sign, a changed polynomial,
an empty graph, a swapped node context and a swapped nested-moment context.

The [context record](data/hexrcf-root-context/) retains validation and
inspected desktop/narrow captures at its recorded source revision. The separate
[root/header record](data/hexrcf-root-header/) retains the canonical-roundtrip
variant and its own source revision. Each verifier checks that record's source.
Failed and preliminary attempts are retained separately from final validation.

These are conformance and proof-construction diagnostics using reused build
dependencies. They establish no compiled adapter performance, scaling or
algebraic completeness result. The optional adapter still needs generic
source/provider/predecessor and fully frozen-context assembly, complete-cell
tactic quotation, the accepted algebraic-completeness integration, remaining
conversions/joint realization and required evidence. Strict compiled missing
records and inverse/xgcd stress remain existing owner requests. #10358 stays
open until its entire acceptance scope is delivered.
