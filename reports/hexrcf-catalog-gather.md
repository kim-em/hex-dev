# Automatic validated-base gathering

`Gather.runFrom?` selects the owner's actual shared base from a validated
catalog before transporting coefficients and producing ordinary samples.
Callers retain the original owner order and do not choose a common target.
The public entrypoint composes `Shared.gatherFrom?` and the existing
`Samples.run`; it introduces neither a field instance on stored expressions
nor another formula representation.

`runFrom?_spec` preserves the shared quantified formula under the gathered
owners' real interpretations. `runFrom?_original` uses the actual source-model
factory equations to preserve separately authenticated original coefficient
values, without an independent coefficient-agreement premise.

`gather_catalog` proves that gathering and decision production succeed when
each installed prefix has an authenticated provider model and one installed
prefix admits every original ordered key path. Its ordinary-real conclusion
requires every original base to have depth zero. The proof derives the selected
prefix, constructs its real model, invokes the owner's gathering-success law
and obtains the decision through the existing sample law. It assumes neither
a successful factory equation nor a Boolean answer. The supplied candidate
establishes availability; deterministic selection may choose another admissible
installed prefix. No relative-transcendence premise is manufactured from
separately registered constants.

The [public-import conformance module](../conformance/HexRCF/GatherCatalog.lean)
retains both selected √2 conjugates, an independent √3 and a repeated owner.
Compiled controls cover the further-root existential, an impossible square
equal to negative √2, repeated-owner leading cancellation, the zero sum of the
two √2 roots and empty rational coefficients. An ordinary-kernel refusal law
shows that no jointly admissible installed key path yields no verdict. Complete
axiom inventories of the three new correctness laws and refusal law allow only
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Compiled answers are controls,
not proof evidence for the original source sentence.

The [adapter implementation](../adapters/HexRCF/RealCoefficients/Gather.lean),
existing Lake conformance target and manual preserve the explicit-gathering API
and examples. The manual's √2/√3 computation now uses automatic base selection.
The optional adapter and full manual build pass 14,033 Lake jobs against the
integrated Mathlib and Tau Ceti pins. The
[build context](data/hexrcf-catalog-gather/context.json) retains exact source
bindings and separate failed/corrected diagnostics. These are correctness
builds, not scientific timing or scaling observations.

This is native producer integration. It performs root finding and does not
supply frozen context/root/row assembly, original divisor authentication or a
production-free replay checker. Symbolic infinitesimal bases retain their
separate finite joint-realization obligation. Complete frontend construction
and accepted-certificate progress remain distinct requirements; the
[adapter inventory](hexrcf-adapter-evidence.md) records their remaining scope.
