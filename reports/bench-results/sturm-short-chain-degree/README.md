# Sturm short-chain degree evidence

The declarations and [independent derivation](derivation.md) precede the
measurement on the clean source recorded in [metadata](metadata.json).
`2X^n−1`, query one and endpoints ±1 retain two real roots and a three-entry
chain on the four even degree rungs. The dynamic recurrence, finite endpoint
passes, replay and complete hashes have linear bounded-word work.

[Results](results.json) retain all 208 points across 13 mode-1 cases, with
four fixed trial-major samples at each compiled rung. All pass without signal
trimming or budget truncation. [Analysis](analysis.json) joins the eight
adjacent alternating AB/BA comparison arms: all 16 common-parameter pairs
have matching complete Option Int hashes. Integer queries are faster on this
recorded source and host; the median rational/integer ratios are recorded
without a portable speed claim. Source and executable checks remain unchanged.

The [representative profile](sturm-short-chain.manifest.json) covers the actual
query at degree 65536. It retains 283 operation-window samples, with 0.614493 ms
calibration residual and passing ±5 ms sensitivity checks. Its raw perf/samply
data, kernel sidecar, symbols and exact executable are in persistent storage;
[artifact checks](artifact-check.json) verify every recorded hash. The profile
attributes bounded-scalar normalization and array traversals, with 67.49%
inclusive shared-chain production, 47.70% pseudo-division, 78.45% rational gcd
and 43.11% allocation self share. Inclusive percentages overlap. This is
required representative attribution, not a replacement for timing observations.

This evidence covers a growing degree with fixed short chain and bounded-word
coefficients/endpoints. It does not admit the unchanged failed long-chain or
growing-bit families, expensive extension signs or nested evidence. Phase 4
remains incomplete. The local compiled target and all 57 smoke registrations
pass; the exact fixture formula remains independently validated by the existing
CI wrapper. No Mathlib is imported by this benchmark.
