# Interpreting a reconstructed native root context

`RootContext.packWith` assembles the existing owner's native root constructor
from a closed retained parent and a checked descriptor of its indexed type.
It retains the parent chain and native operations, checks the frame encoding
and child signature, and checks the resulting context in the ordinary kernel.
It adds no field instance to algebraic representatives.

The frame is `Tower.rootData parent.codec descriptor`. The current
`Literal.ofJson` is an identity conversion, so its encoding equation can be
checked without evaluating that serialization. The frame remains an expression,
not an independently frozen numerical history. This construction does not
authenticate an arbitrary source alias, registered provider or origin history.

`SignatureQuote.decoder` consumes the owner's public `Signature.codec_literal`
equation and an independently checked full-literal binding. It quotes the exact
decoder subject after reducing a graph-field projection, without running the
signature parser during proof construction. Two distinct signatures test the
binding, wrong subjects, full caller rollback and exact/one-below budgets.
The public cubic graph retains only numerical JSON constructors, including
node and nested-moment contexts for its rational predecessor. This does not
freeze arbitrary root frames in a deeper tower or validate a registry/history.

The public regression packs the root reconstructed by `RootHeader.quoteWith`,
then builds its ordinary-real interpretation using the existing `RealModel.build`
and `Tower.Model.root_value` APIs. The already decoded coefficient has exactly
the same selected real value in this packed context and remains positive.
The context is exported as computable native data; its real model is explicitly
noncomputable. No compiled result supplies either mathematical proof.

The packing request charges 2,653 proof nodes. Its returned usage is checked
against the enclosing request, and exact and one-node-below budgets are tested.
This count covers packing and its context/signature proofs. It excludes the
separate model construction, preceding root/header and scalar requests, and
source-goal reification. It is not a full tactic or unique-DAG measurement.

A descriptor for the parent carrier is rejected when passed to the child
carrier. That failure exercises caller assignments and a temporary declaration,
then checks full metavariable and declaration restoration. An unresolved root
input rejects without assigning caller metavariables. These are terminal
refusals; no alternative solver is selected.

The fresh importing audit checks 718 theorem bodies and 68 definitions across
its explicitly listed source scope, with 771 nonempty diagnostic traces and
complete transitive axiom closure restricted to `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound`. It includes the metadata, signature, model, value and positive
conclusions and the original packet/coefficient reader conclusions. The shared
native-producer guard is unchanged. New packing-module bodies additionally
reject root-data serialization and DAG encoding reductions. The source lists
its fixture and adapter modules explicitly; it does not
recheck every body in the owner companion libraries. The axiom closure remains
transitive across those libraries.

A separate fresh importing audit checks 23 theorem bodies and seven definitions
for the two frozen signature subjects and the projected-constructor regression,
with 16 nonempty diagnostic traces. Its complete axiom closure is `propext` and
`Quot.sound`; its producer guard also excludes serialization and DAG encoding.
Direct signature requests charge 254 proof nodes each; the projected request

These counts cover distinct requests, without a claim about unique DAG nodes
or total runtime work.

These are local build-only conformance and proof-construction observations,
without compiled runtime, scaling or speedup claims. Source-bound records
retain failed and preliminary attempts separately from final validation in
the [retained record](data/hexrcf-root-context/) at its recorded source revision.
The optional adapter still requires general original-source/provider/predecessor

of the required acceptance evidence. Ordinary-model construction here does
not supply a real interpretation of an infinitesimal or a finite-joint
