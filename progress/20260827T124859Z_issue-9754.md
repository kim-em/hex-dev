# HexPrimality runtime-route review (#9754)

Session type: independent Phase-2 skeptical review.

## Audited routes

- `MillerRabin.lean`: checked the exact size/parity/base/gcd branch order,
  odd decomposition, strong-witness squaring window, the compositeness theorem,
  default base list, and the absence of a false probable-prime theorem. The
  algorithm and one-way proof contract are genuine.
- `PMinusOne.lean`: checked prime-power exponent construction against the
  capped effective bound, modular subtraction, initial and terminal gcds, all
  three outcomes, the cap equality theorem, and the dynamically validated
  proper-divisor theorem. The stage-1 primitive is genuine.
- `Search.lean`: checked Brent power-of-two anchors, 32-difference batching,
  cycle-boundary flushes, whole-modulus replay, fixed-point/degenerate draw
  rejection, eight-restart policy, success validation, failure states,
  partial-product preservation, recursive certificate assembly, checked
  acceptance, table/trial/Miller--Rabin/certificate dispatch, attempt and Rand
  propagation, default fuel, total trial fallback, and bounded next-prime
  minimality.
- Downstream boundary: checked `HexIntFactor/Rho.lean`,
  `HexIntFactor/PMinusOne.lean`, and the corresponding `Factor.lean` dispatch
  use of counted successes, proper-divisor theorems, restart caps, and
  subsequent p-1/ECM reachability.

## Blockers

- #9781: deterministic table/Miller--Rabin verdicts are incorrectly gated by
  certificate fuel and become the wrong `.exhausted` stop at fuel zero.
- #9782: rho and witness draws bypass bounded multiword rejection sampling;
  ranges truncate above 64 bits, modulo bias remains, and rho substitutes a
  non-drawn fallback pair after rejection exhaustion.
- #9783: the shared p-1 primitive is absent from certificate
  `partialFactor` and lacks the promised counted/resumable shared shape; the
  SPEC's private partial-search signature is also stale.
- #9784: `defaultPrimeFuel` is explicitly documented as an unmeasured starting
  point and is not covered by #9757's existing threshold-policy work. It must
  follow #9781 because the fixed-front-end boundary changes fuel semantics.
- #9785: the runtime routes recompute `oddSplit`, `divOut`, `Rand.next`, and
  `rhoInnerFuel`; the recursive `divOut` source call tree can be exponential in
  a factor's valuation unless compiler common-subexpression elimination saves
  it.
- #9799: `nextPrime?` preserves advanced Rand but drops the failing
  candidate's internal certificate-attempt subtotal, while its `attempts`
  field actually counts conclusively rejected candidates in a different unit.

All six issues are dependencies of the Phase-2 attestation #9759. No review
token or registry counter was changed.

## Focused evidence

A temporary imported probe module was built and then removed. It observed:

- `primeCert? 4 seed 0` and `primeCert? 2147483649 seed 0` return
  `.exhausted`, zero attempts, and unchanged Rand at fuel zero, but
  `.composite` at fuel one; `isPrime? 2147483649 seed 0` has the same split.
- `rhoDrawTrace (2^80) seed0` returned the word-sized pair
  `(16294208416658607536, 7960286522194355700)`.
- p-1 produced `whole`, `noFactor`, and a proper factor `13` on focused cases.
- The probe reconfirmed existing Brent guards: recovery returned factor `3`
  for modulus `9`, and the batched trace performed 95 steps with seven gcds.
- Existing guards cover table/trial/certificate dispatch, exact counted
  recursive successes/failures, rho rejection/recovery/batching, total
  fallback spot values, and next-prime minimality across the table edge.
