# Specialized polynomial dispatch experiment

## Accomplished

- Added a Mathlib-free, operation-key-based univariate integer-polynomial
  recognizer under the planned `HexIntervalAlgebraic` integration owner.
- Routed `x^3 - 2` through the real Sturm isolator/refiner and
  `x^3 - x - 1` through the complex NK/Pellet isolator.
- Added exact graph/payload replay schemas backed by
  `RealRootIsolations.isolates` and the certified Pisot complex-root coverage,
  then closed both through the generic proof frontend as ordinary theorems.
- Added rejection guards for non-polynomial graphs, variable-key, operand-order,
  coefficient, endpoint, and region mutations.
- Documented the bounded coverage and remaining general-dispatch limitations in
  the interval Mathlib SPEC.
- Built the focused runtime/proof/conformance modules and the full
  `HexIntervalMathlibExperiment` and `HexConformance` targets; DAG, line-count,
  copyright, trust-surface, and benchmark-freshness checks pass.

## Current frontier

The dispatcher recognizes one distinguished variable with registered integer
constants and `+`, `-`, `*`. Provider result schemas remain deliberately finite
to the two canaries.

## Next step

Generalize coefficient and result-fact schemas, add normalized/shared graph
recognition, and measure routing against general interval propagation before
promoting the planned degree-five algebraic acceptance fixtures.

## Blockers

None. The complex isolation API and its Mathlib proof boundary both exist.
