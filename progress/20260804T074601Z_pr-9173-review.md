# PR #9173 review — iterated quadratic-norm certificate

## Accomplished

Reviewed the paper theorem, executable certificate, recovery prototype, service
timing spans, both Python drivers, and the committed measurement records at
`e4796978`. No mathematical counterexample and no reducible polynomial accepted
by `Certificate.check` were found. Verified the target build and reran all 15
directed witnesses plus the 250-case randomized comparison successfully.

Independently reconstructed the baseline cactus ratios and the post-prime
counterfactual. The published `1.188x` baseline and approximately `0.811x`
modeled worst ratio are arithmetically reproducible from the pinned sweeps,
certificate record, published replacement times, and retained normalization,
quadratic-probe, and prime-walk phases.

## Current frontier

The principal review finding is measurement-policy specification: the report
does not define the predicate or threshold behind its “budget-gated” placement,
does not commit the per-row retained phase observations or a simulation script,
and the modeled set includes `sd4` despite describing cheap recombination rows
as paying no recognizer overhead. The existing 262,144-node hard budget would
also not admit `sd5`'s 32,768-node walk, so the exact gate matters.

Secondary findings: the speedup driver records certificate spans from a
certificate-only process while retaining only the production span from the
paired process; the report's `±`-monic normalization is not implemented by the
prototype; sweep selection/cutoff behavior is under-specified; and the updated
witness record has no environment provenance despite the report claiming both
records pin revision `37b9062b`.

## Next step
