# hex-primality: the cube-root arm wired (milestone 4 complete)

## Accomplished

- `checkPock3Arith` replaced its stub with the real
  Brillhart-Lehmer-Selfridge conditions: the square-root arm's checks
  minus the `n < F * F` bound, plus `F` even, `R` odd, the
  `R = 2Fs + r` decomposition with `1 ≤ r < 2F`, the cube-root size
  bound, and the discriminant disjunction verified through the stored
  witness `w` (two multiplications; when `r² ≤ 8s` truncation makes
  the witness clause unsatisfiable and the middle disjunct covers the
  region).
- The per-entry witness derivation is factored into
  `pock_divisor_step`, shared by both soundness cases; the `pock3`
  case of `prime_of_checkPrime_aux` now closes through `pocklington3`.
- `primeCertGo` routes: `.pock` when the factored part clears the
  square root, `mkPock3` (runtime `Nat.sqrt` for the witness)
  otherwise; the wrapper's `checkPrime` validation decides acceptance,
  so the search adds no second set of checks. `primeCertGo_composite`
  extended over the new arms (all new exits are exhaustion or success).
- Guards: the `n = 199, F = 6` cube-root certificate accepted
  (F² = 36 ≤ 199, discriminant 65 with witness 8), and rejections for
  a wrong witness, `r` out of range, odd `F`, and the same data on the
  square-root arm; `mkPock3` assembly cross-checked against the
  checker.
- SPEC corrections recorded: "except step 5" → "except step 4" (the
  earlier text would have been unsound), the stored-witness
  discriminant test replacing the non-kernel-reducible `Nat.sqrt`,
  the `pock3` constructor signature, and `Nat.sqrt` removed from the
  documented replay closure.
