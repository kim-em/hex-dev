# Issue #8852 symmetry-aware integer Gram rows

## Accomplished

- Claimed issue #8852 and rebased `issue-8852` onto current `origin/main`.
- Confirmed the generic `gramMatrix` remains outside the sound optimization
  surface and that the two integer runtime paths construct Gram rows
  independently.
- Added isolated Gram-construction and consumer benchmarks for square and wide
  lattice shapes; both benchmark executables compile.
- Recorded matched Gram–Schmidt baselines: construction is about 45% of the
  square Schur consumer at `n = 32` and 81–88% of the wide consumer at common
  rungs, decisively clearing the Amdahl gate.
- Split the interval probes into a focused `hexlll_gram_bench` executable after
  confirming that unrelated `hexlll_bench` expected-hash initializers consume
  roughly 80 seconds before a 9.4 µs `n = 3` requested probe.
- Recorded matched interval baselines: construction is 28–48% of the square
  checker and 58–71% of the wide checker, so this distinct path also clears
  the Amdahl gate.
- Added `gramRowsImpl`, which computes only `i ≤ j` dot products and mirrors
  the cached upper triangle, with `@[csimp] gramRows_eq_impl` proved from
  integer dot-product commutativity.
- Routed `lllReducedInterval` through `gramRows`; the adjusted Mathlib
  soundness proof builds without changing the generic `gramMatrix` surface.
- Recorded clean matched after measurements. Square/wide `gramRows` improved
  by 22.6%/42.7%, the square/wide Schur consumers by 11.2%/32.5%, and the
  square/wide interval checker by 10.3%/30.0% at their largest benchmark
  rungs. All result-hash ladders match their baselines.
- Added the committed artefacts, checksums, Amdahl shares, and before/after
  tables to the Gram–Schmidt and LLL performance reports.
