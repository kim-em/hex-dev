# HexNumberField normalized inversion extended gcd

- Added `DensePoly.xgcdLeftMonic`, which rescales each nonzero remainder and
  its tracked left Bezout coefficient before the next division while leaving
  `xgcdLeft` unchanged for exact-cofactor callers.
- Proved divisibility and Bezout contracts over fields and used them to carry
  the `QAdjoin.inv` field-law proof in `HexNumberFieldMathlib`.
- Switched `QAdjoin.inv` to the normalized chain. The conformance fixtures are
  byte-for-byte unchanged, and a compiled regression compares the normalized
  and legacy inverse representatives directly.
- Corrected the inversion ladder's declared bit-cost upper bound to
  `n³ log n`: `O(n²)` rational coefficient operations on `O(n log n)`-bit
  coefficients. The five-trial scientific rerun is faster than that upper
  bound (`beta = -0.721`), with a 1.348 ms median at `n = 20` versus 3.248 ms
  for the shipped chain's idle-host run.
- Verified the combined `HexPoly`, `HexNumberField`, and
  `HexNumberFieldMathlib` build, NumberField conformance compilation, unchanged
  emitted fixtures, Lean-native benchmark fast checks, and Phase-4 checks.
  Local PARI checks await CI because `cypari2` is not installed on the host.
