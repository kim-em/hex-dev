# 2026-08-04 — issue #9154, Hensel product-tree cost

## Accomplished

**Measurement.** Added `henselTreeProfile` to `hexbz_factor_service`: a mirror
of `multifactorLiftQuadraticListImpl` that times every node's sub-products,
XGCD and doubling steps, checks its own output against
`multifactorLiftQuadratic`, and replays bignum steps piece by piece. The
node-level sum accounts for 101-104% of the untimed lift and, with the tier's
other timed phases, for the whole proposal tier on wilkinson 40/48/56. Findings
are written up in `reports/hensel-product-tree-node-profile.md`.

The headline finding corrects the framing in the issue: the products are not the
cost. Kronecker already handles them and they are under a tenth of a bignum
doubling step. The two **monic modular divisions** were 55.5% of it, because
`divModMonicModSquareAux` rebuilt and re-canonicalised the entire running
remainder and quotient at each of `deg f` elimination steps although each step
changes only `deg q + 1` slots. Coefficient width barely moves the cost;
coefficient *count* and allocation do.

**Implementation** — one strategy: stop touching, copying and re-canonicalising
coefficients the step does not change.

1. `ZPoly.intEmod` — the `Int`-valued canonicaliser, with a `@[csimp]`
   implementation that returns an already-canonical value *as itself* (no
   allocation) and never routes a wide value through the `Int`/`Nat` round trip
   `intModNat` forced.
2. `elimStep` / `quotStep` — windowed, in-place remainder elimination and
   quotient write, with `divModMonicModSquareAuxImpl` and a `@[csimp]` proof
   that the windowed loop equals the specification loop on monic divisors at a
   positive modulus (guarded fall-back to the specification loop otherwise).
3. `quadraticHenselStepBignumImpl` / `quadraticHenselFactorsBignumImpl` — narrow
   the step's target to its own modulus before forming the residual, and fuse
   the nested `addModSquare`/`subModSquare` chains so each update canonicalises
   once instead of twice.

No `axiom`, `sorry`, `native_decide` or `@[implemented_by]`; every swap is a
`@[csimp]` equality proved on all inputs.

`HexHensel/Quadratic.lean` would have passed the 3000-line cap, so the modular
coefficient kernel and the division moved to `HexHensel/ModularDivision.lean`
(650 lines), which `Quadratic.lean` now imports.

## Current frontier

The divisions are now close to their arithmetic floor: what remains is
`deg f · deg q` genuine coefficient multiply-subtract-reduce triples. Another
large step needs a different algorithm, not a tighter loop.

## Next step

Two candidates, both bigger than this issue:

* **Halve every bignum width in the step.** `e = f - g·h` is divisible by `m`;
  running the whole correction on `e/m` at modulus `m` rather than `m²` halves
  every operand. It needs a runtime divisibility guard (the identity is false
  without the Hensel invariant) plus a re-derivation of the correction algebra
  under that guard.
* **Kronecker/Newton division.** A reversed-series inverse of `g` turns the
  `Θ(deg f · deg q)` elimination into three multiplications. This is the real
  asymptotic fix and the reason the divisions still lead the profile, but it
  needs a power-series-inversion theory the project does not have.
