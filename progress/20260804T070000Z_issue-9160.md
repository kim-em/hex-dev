# Issue #9160: attribute the packed Berlekamp kernel's 2.6x gap

## Accomplished

Measured the gap the integration report named by inspection, on
`chungus2` at revision `fca8ee4e`, 24 corpus rows, three repeats merged by
median, every rung checked against `Hex.Matrix.nullspace`.

Three instruments in `bench/HexBench/BerlekampKernel.lean`:

- `countedPackedReduce`, a counted mirror of `Packed.reduceLoop` marking the
  clock once per pivot column, checked against production `Packed.reduce`.
- `ladderLoop`, the same loop with its column elimination as a parameter, plus
  `eliminateHoisted` and `eliminateFlat`, so each rung drops one scaffolding
  choice; the bottom rung is the prototype's `reduce32` on the packed buffer.
- `eliminateFlatDoubleMul`/`eliminateFlatSalted`, which price the inner modular
  multiply by performing a second salted multiply per entry and combining by
  `min`.

Result: the per-row-addition `Hex.Matrix.getRow` copy is 70.1% of the
integrated reduction on `cyclo_phi385` and 71.3% on `cyclo_phi128_x_phi165`.
`Matrix.modifyEntries`'s closure is not a cost on those rows, and the
`List.finRange` scaffolding is a cost only where the reduction is dominated by
already-zero columns. The inner multiply is at least 7.8% of the shipped
reduction, 26.0% of what it becomes without the copy, at 0.78 ns median. Both
open design points close: the same run prices `UInt64` against `UInt32` at
median 1.61x for the shipped choice, and the hardware remainder against Barrett
at median 0.983 with the remainder faster on 20 of 24 rows.

Report: `reports/hexbz-packed-kernel-attribution.md`, reading
`reports/bench-results/hexbz-phase-profile-fca8ee4e-chungus2.json`. PR #9165.

## Current frontier

The let-floating trap is the one that bit: the first stage mirror charged 236
columns of elimination 11 us against a 46 ms wall, because the eliminated
buffer's only consumer is the tail call. The generic `countedRowReduce` never
had the problem because its elimination returns a pair and the destructuring
match cannot be floated. Anything mirroring a loop whose step returns a single
value needs an explicit force before the mark.

## Next step

The optimization this points at is deliberately not in this PR: hoist the pivot
row read to one per pivot column in `Packed.eliminateColumn` and thread it into
`Packed.rowAdd`. `eliminateHoisted` is the shape; the proof cost is one more
invariant in `rep_eliminateColumn_foldl`. Two secondary findings belong with
it: `Packed.nullspaceArray`'s list walk (11x to 53x the array scan, a fifth to a
third of the whole span on high-nullity rows) and the per-column `List.finRange`
scaffolding (18% to 35% on skip-dominated rows).
