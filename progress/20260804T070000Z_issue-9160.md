# Issue #9160: attribute the packed Berlekamp kernel's 2.6x gap

## Accomplished

Measured the gap the integration report and the issue named by inspection, on
`chungus2` at revision `49e211a0`, 24 corpus rows, seven repeats with the ladder
run in alternating directions, every difference a median of within-repeat paired
differences reported with its range. Every rung checked against
`Hex.Matrix.nullspace`.

Three instruments in `bench/HexBench/BerlekampKernel.lean`:

- `countedPackedReduce`, a counted mirror of `Packed.reduceLoop` marking the
  clock once per pivot column, checked against production `Packed.reduce`, with
  the row-addition counting pass marked separately because production has none.
- `ladderLoop` and `ladderLoopArray` plus `eliminateHoisted`, `eliminateFlatRow`
  and `eliminateFlat`, so each rung changes exactly one thing; the prototype's
  `reduce32` on the packed buffer is the residual comparison, not an attribution
  rung.
- `eliminateFlatSaltedMin`/`eliminateFlatDoubleMul` and the `modifyEntries`-shape
  pair, which price the inner modular multiply against a control that carries
  the same compare and select.

Result: the per-row-addition `Hex.Matrix.getRow` copy is 71.3% of the integrated
reduction on `cyclo_phi385`, 70.8% on `cyclo_phi128_x_phi165`, 65.9% on
`randprod_21`. Every other candidate is under 1% there, and
`Matrix.modifyEntries` is 2.5% *faster* than a flat write. The `List.finRange`
column scan is 15% to 24% on the rows dominated by already-zero columns. The
multiply is 4.8% of the shipped reduction, 16.9% of the copy-free one. Neither
open design point warrants a shipped-loop variant.

Report: `reports/hexbz-packed-kernel-attribution.md`. PR #9165. Follow-up
issue #9166.

## Current frontier

Two measurement traps bit and both are now documented in the report. Let-floating
charged 236 columns of elimination 11 us against a 46 ms wall, because the
eliminated buffer's only consumer is the tail call; the generic
`countedRowReduce` never had the problem because its elimination returns a pair
and the destructuring match cannot be floated. And the first version of the
multiply probe charged the compare and select to the multiply, which doubled its
apparent cost: on `cyclo_phi385` the select alone is 1.515 ms against the
multiply's 1.479 ms.
