# BZ recombination: filter supports before materializing them (issue #9129)

## Accomplished

The head-forced classical traversal no longer builds anything a rejected
support does not need.

- `Hex.SupportMeta` records, once per subset-cardinality level, the lift
  modulus, the modulus as an `Int`, and each lifted factor's degree and
  trailing coefficient. Its four proof fields pin every recorded value to the
  lifted factor it describes, so the iterator theorems now hold for *any*
  metadata record rather than only the canonical `Hex.supportMeta`.
- `Hex.directLeaf` runs `directCandidatePrefilter` before reversing the
  selected prefix, before mapping it to lifted polynomials, and before
  concatenating the complementary support (which is built only on success).
  `Hex.directLeaf_eq` states that the guarded leaf is the old leaf; the
  prefilter is `tryDirectCandidate`'s own first step, so guarding on it changes
  only when the arguments are built.
- `scanDirectSubsets` (the unforced proposal-tier sweep) got the same
  treatment, and the counted mirror in `bench/HexBench/FactorService.lean` was
  moved to the new materialization point so it keeps measuring the code that
  runs.
- No new `sorry` or `axiom`; `check_trust_surface.py` passes on all 408
  published files. `scanDirectCombinations_found` and
  `scanDirectCombinations_finds` are unchanged in content, only generalized
  over the metadata record.

Measured on `chungus2` against the #9127 baseline:

- Swinnerton-Dyer end-to-end factor time is **0.684x to 0.850x** across the
  family: `sd5_shift1` 0.684x, `sd5_shift2` 0.687x, `sd5` 0.719x,
  `sd5_x_phi11` 0.734x, `sd5_x_phi45` 0.781x, `sd4_x_sd4shift1` 0.850x.
- Traversal outside the candidate test falls **42.2%** on `sd5` and **46.9%**
  on `sd5_x_phi11`; whole-factorization allocator time falls **30.6%** and
  **30.7%**.
- No other corpus row moves more than 2.4%.
- All recombination stage counters and all returned factor degrees are
  identical before and after; the counted mirror agrees with the production
  trace on 367 of 367 validation rows.
- The SD family's median against verified Isabelle BZ improves from `2.799x`
  to `2.116x`.

The report is [reports/hexbz-support-traversal.md](../reports/hexbz-support-traversal.md).

## Current frontier

The issue also proposed a `SupportCursor` over a compact array of increasing
indices, replacing the list traversal outright. The measurements do not support
that, and the report says so with evidence: with the leaf guarded, the spine
costs two constructor allocations per internal node and Lean's small-object
allocator is 10.1% of `sd5`'s remaining total, while the residual leaf cost is
93-bit modular arithmetic (`centeredModNat` alone is 7.9% of total). The
representation is not what is left to win; the modular arithmetic is, and that
is #9130.

## Next step

#9130 (reject impossible exact divisors over a word-sized prime) is unblocked
by this and is where the remaining recombination cost now lives. The residual
`sd5` profile in the report is its starting point.

## Blockers

None for this issue, but a measurement hazard worth passing on: the host is
shared with the agents working on #9128 and #9131, and the issue text tells
every one of us to pin to CPU 0. Three factorization services on one core gave
sweeps with a median of 1.10x and outliers to 6.8x on rows that cannot have
changed. The committed records are pinned to CPU 70 instead, and the
substitution is validated -- 354 unaffected corpus rows have median 0.9977 and
p90 1.0234 against the baseline. Repeat that validation rather than trusting a
single run.

## Addendum after second-opinion review

Codex review of PR #9138 found no correctness regression but four fair points,
three of which are fixed here:

- a surviving leaf evaluated `directCandidatePrefilter` twice, once in
  `directLeaf`'s guard and again as `tryDirectCandidate`'s first step, which
  also meant the counted bench mirror was no longer an operational mirror.
  `directCandidateAfterPrefilter` is now the shared post-filter body;
- `SupportMeta` gained `degrees_size` and `trails_size`, because without them a
  too-short array satisfies the elementwise equations whenever the omitted
  entries happen to be zero, so the docstring claimed more than the fields did;
- two docstrings overclaimed: `LiftData` does not encode primality, and a
  rejected leaf still pays for the filters' own modular arithmetic.

The fourth, hoisting the metadata from per-level to per-search, is declined and
documented in the report: it is 16 constructions against 32,768 leaves on `sd5`
and would have to thread through `findDirectHead` and `searchDirectAux`, whose
signatures the completeness proofs consume. A suggested rename to
`DirectSupportMeta` is also declined; it pushed several `simp only` lists past
the file's line width for no gain, and the project's naming guidance prefers
the shorter form. All measurements were re-taken at the corrected revision.
