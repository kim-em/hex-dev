# Accomplished

- Remeasured the Berlekamp fixed-space kernel after #9128 and reported a **go**
  on issue #9132's measurement threshold. Row reduction and nullspace basis at
  the selected prime are 46.1% of total factor time on `cyclo_phi385`, 44.1% on
  `cyclo_phi128_x_phi165`, 27.2% on `cyclo_phi64_x_phi105`, and 76.1% of the
  Berlekamp split on `cyclo_phi179`.
- Added `--entry kernelProfile` to `hexbz_factor_service` and
  `bench/HexBench/BerlekampKernel.lean`: a stage-by-stage attribution of one
  kernel computation, a counted Gauss-Jordan mirror of `Hex.Matrix.rowReduce`'s
  private loop, and four packed prototype variants. Every packed result is
  checked entry for entry against `Hex.Matrix.nullspace` before its time is
  reported; the driver exits non-zero on disagreement.
- Extended `scripts/bench/factor_phase_profile.py` with a `kernels` section
  (schema `hexbz-phase-profile/3`), and wrote
  `reports/hexbz-packed-fp-matrix.md` from
  `reports/bench-results/hexbz-phase-profile-47aa7048-chungus2.json`.
- Priced the packed representation: 16.8x on `cyclo_phi385`'s row reduction
  including packing, projecting 1.72x end to end there and 1.13x to 1.67x on the
  other named cyclotomic rows. Decomposed into transform removal (2.00x),
  contiguous buffer with the modulus in a register (4.08x), immediate `UInt32`
  entries (2.42x), and Barrett against hardware remainder (1.01x).
- Ran an independent Codex review, which found three real defects, all fixed:
  the packed loops skipped zero source entries the generic path does not skip;
  the rebuild forcing barrier did not survive common-subexpression elimination,
  so the second and later matrix builds measured 130 ns and were charged to the
  stage under test; and the threshold numerator counted Berlekamp matrix
  construction, which packing does not accelerate.
- Refreshed the full Hex factor sweep and regenerated all 25 factorization
  figures; freshness, plot, DAG, line-count, copyright, bench-Mathlib-free,
  conformance-matrix, release-manifest, trust-surface, and phase-4 checks pass.
- Opened PR #9148 and filed successor issue #9150 carrying the settled design,
  the proof obligations, and the four measurement traps.

# Blockers

None. The report's "What the measurements do not establish" section lists the
limits of the evidence: the two sections supplying numerator and denominator are
not paired observations, the medians are field-wise across three repeats, the
prototype carries no correspondence proof, and the corpus moduli are far below
the `2^31` bound the representation must support. All four should be closed by
measuring the integrated implementation rather than by re-running this
diagnostic.
