# Brown--Traub column transformation

## Accomplished

- Added the Brown multiplier-column transformation for generalized Sylvester
  coefficient matrices and proved that it preserves the local determinant.
- Proved the integer-indexed coefficient convolution, including negative and
  out-of-range indices, and allowed multipliers whose stored size is only
  bounded by the formal multiplier degree.
- Proved `productCols_addMul`, `det_addMul`, and `coeffMinorAt_addMul`, the
  column-operation form of Brown--Traub equation (18).
- Added an odd-sign, positive-`J`, degree-dropping entrywise conformance pin and
  documented the transformation in the Resultant SPEC and manual chapter.
- Incorporated an independent review: reused the existing coefficient-matrix
  API, removed a duplicate generic layer, weakened the multiplier-size premise,
  and clarified that formal-degree collapse is the next transformation.
- Verified `HexResultant.BrownTraub`, Resultant conformance, the Resultant manual
  chapter, and a full 9,567-job `lake build`.

## Current frontier

The swapped `G, F` generalized Sylvester matrix is now transformed into the
ordinary explicit-degree `G, H` matrix for `H = F + B * G`, with exactly the
block-swap sign and no new `sorry` or axiom.

## Next step

Merge this milestone, then prove the formal-degree collapse that contributes
the leading-coefficient power and combine it with scaling to derive the
Brown--Traub factorization and coefficientwise integrality laws.
