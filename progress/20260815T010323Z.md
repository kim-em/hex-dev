# FKS2 shard scale probe

## Accomplished

- Added a Mathlib-free exact-rational provider for the first 128 cells of pinned
  FKS2 extended-Table-4 shard 11, scheduled as eight bounded 16-cell actions.
- Proved the shared Taylor remainder and all 128 cell obligations in the
  Mathlib companion, including the source-shaped boolean-all theorem and an
  arbitrary-membership semantic wrapper.
- Added indexed replay, exact chronology/resource guards, and a false-cell
  mutation that fails at coordinate `11010` without allocating a draft or
  retrying precision.
- Recorded the probe as partial `128/13590` coverage in the SPEC and pinned
  inventory, retaining the generated-family status as `pending`.
- Verified exact tuple correspondence with the first 128 pinned source cells,
  focused and full builds, factor freshness, inventory/source checks, and the
  published trust surface.

## Current frontier

The representative production-scale chunk is complete. It demonstrates the
runtime action envelope and direct indexed replay for 128 cells, but deliberately
does not claim the remaining 872 cells of shard 11 or the other 12,590 cells.

## Next step

Generic `ProofFrontend` closure over this large payload remains an explicit
future acceptance item rather than a claim of this probe.
