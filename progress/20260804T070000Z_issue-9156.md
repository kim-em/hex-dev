# Prime-plan pricing (issue #9156)

## Accomplished

Replaced the direct prime planner's fixed `scoutWidth = 8` stop threshold with
`Hex.scoutPays`, one deterministic comparison between the recombination work the
plan in hand still has to do and the scouts plus split that replacing it would
cost. Both sides are estimated from shape already observed -- input degree,
primes, degree multisets -- and share a factor `n^2` which cancels, so the rule
is one inequality with two calibrated integer ratios.

- `HexBerlekampZassenhaus/Modular/PrimePlan.lean`: `liftWords`,
  `scoutRoundCost`, `splitColumnCost`, `ScoutIncumbent`, `scoutPays`.
  `scoutBetterPattern` now carries the incumbent plan rather than a bare width,
  and `firstDirectPlan?` lost its width branch. Both `DirectPrimePlan` facts
  stay proved and their proofs got shorter by one case each.
- `scripts/bench/prime_policy_replay.py`: offline replay of eight policies over
  recorded per-candidate costs, plus the decision-margin table and a check that
  the replayed pre-change policy reproduces the recorded binary's selection.
- `scripts/bench/prime_plan_paired.py`: names the measured core to its child
  (`--cpu auto` in the child was resetting the inherited affinity, so the two
  arms could land on different cores), and takes `--changed` because a dropped
  scout moves neither the selected prime nor the split count.
- `bench/HexBench/FactorService.lean`: the counterfactual's fixed comparison set
  is pinned with its own historical constants; scout rows carry `liftWords`,
  `recombUnits` and `splitMaxDegree`.
- Conformance guards pin both directions of `scoutPays` and the modulus width.
- `reports/hexbz-prime-plan-pricing.md`, and the SPEC's prime-selection section.

The constants are the measured medians over the 144 priced candidates of the
recorded per-candidate profile: a scout word operation runs 10x a recombination
one, a split word operation 5x. An earlier eyeballed 12 and 3 gave identical
decisions on the corpus and was replaced because the medians are auditable.

## Current frontier

`cyclo_phi64_x_phi105` is the one row on the corpus whose walk changes: prime
walk 37.699 ms to 10.676 ms (-71.7%), total 45.493 ms to 18.662 ms (-59.0%),
paired over three alternating rounds against a load control of 0.990x to 1.023x.
Every other row, including the mandatory `xpow105_minus1` control, selects the
same prime and performs the same splits and scouts.

The decision-margin table in the report is the evidence that agreement is a
derivation and not luck: all eleven declines and ten of the thirteen acceptances
are independently confirmed by the measured recombination and scout-plus-split
columns.

## Next step

The three Wilkinson rows are where the `2^(w-1)` upper bound is loosest -- their
recombination is 2.7x to 4.3x below the observation the rule buys, because every
linear factor is a rational root and the search terminates in its first
cardinality level. No function of the degree multiset can separate that from
`sd5`, whose identical-looking image is irreducible and does exhaust the search.
Tightening it needs evidence about reducibility, not a better cost model. Those
rows are unchanged from the fixed threshold, so nothing regresses.

Per the issue's last dependency note, `powModMonicAux` work belongs in a
separate kernel issue opened from the post-change profile.

## Blockers

None. The measurement protocol needs the tree clean while the sweep runs: the
freshness check rejects a record whose `git_dirty` is true, and an editing
session that writes a report during a sweep invalidates it.
