# Prime-plan pricing (issue #9156)

## Accomplished

Replaced the direct prime planner's fixed `scoutWidth = 8` stop threshold with
`Hex.scoutPays`, one deterministic comparison between the recombination work the
plan in hand may still have to do and the scouts plus split the rest of the walk
may spend. Both sides are worst cases over shape already observed, and share a
factor `n^2` which cancels, so the rule is one inequality with two calibrated
integer ratios.

- `HexBerlekampZassenhaus/Modular/PrimePlan.lean`: `liftWords`,
  `scoutRoundCost`, `splitColumnCost`, `ScoutIncumbent` with `maxDegree` and
  `candidatesLeft`, and `scoutPays`. `scoutBetterPattern` carries the incumbent
  plan rather than a bare width; `firstDirectPlan?` lost its width branch. Both
  `DirectPrimePlan` facts stay proved, one case shorter each.
- `defaultSubsetBudget` moved from `Classical/Search.lean` to
  `FactorizationResult.lean`: the planner prices what the engine spends, and
  `Classical.Search` is downstream of `Modular.PrimePlan`.
- `scripts/bench/prime_policy_replay.py`: replays eight policies over recorded
  per-candidate costs, prints every `scoutPays` decision against the best
  attainable net gain, sweeps the two ratios, and checks itself against the
  measured binary's selection.
- `scripts/bench/factor_sweep_paired.py`: whole-corpus before/after in
  counterbalanced AB/BA blocks, per-instance medians of within-block ratios,
  coverage as a set difference.
- `scripts/bench/prime_plan_paired.py`: counterbalanced blocks, paired
  within-block ratios, explicit `--changed` set, and the measured core named to
  the child (its `--cpu auto` was resetting the inherited affinity).
- Conformance guards, the SPEC's prime-selection section, and
  `reports/hexbz-prime-plan-pricing.md`.

## Current frontier

Seven corpus instances change their walk and all seven improve: paired and
counterbalanced on the rebased baseline, `cyclo_phi105_x_phi128` 0.409x,
`cyclo_phi64_x_phi105` 0.414x, `sd4_x_phi17` 0.441x, `legendre_P18` 0.598x,
`legendre_P20` 0.667x, `cyclo_phi24_x_phi35` 0.753x, `cyclo_phi275` 0.792x,
against a load control of 0.985x to 1.003x. Coverage is the same 377 of 392 in
both arms of every block and no instance above 1.10x costs more than 2 ms.

All eight decisions the walk takes on those seven rows are confirmed against the
best attainable net gain over reachable alternatives. Three of the seven have no
better prime reachable at all.

The branch was rebased onto `747d36ed` (which speeds up completely split
finite-field inputs) part-way through, so every number was re-measured with the
before arm built from that commit. The conclusions reproduced; the ratio
calibration moved from 12.9/4.3 to 13.3/4.4 and the in-sample sensitivity from
116/120 to 113/120 once `legendre_P20` joined the recorded set.

## Next step

Second opinion (Codex) found two false claims in the first version of this work,
both now corrected: `liftWords` estimated the modulus as `precision * bitLen p`
and was a word too wide on three rows, and the recombination estimate ignored
`defaultSubsetBudget`. It also caught that the sensitivity sweep compared only
first decisions on one record; over both records and the whole walk it is 116 of
120, not 120 of 120, with all four exceptions at `splitColumnCost = 2` and all
worse than shipped.

What the work does *not* establish, recorded in the report: the rule is an
affordability gate rather than a marginal value-of-information calculation; the
scout-depth proxy has no bound behind it and errs toward under-pricing the scouts
most likely to help; the robustness sweep is in-sample; and the counterfactual
downstream prices the direct route only. Tightening the estimate needs an
expected-cost model validated on held-out families -- the degree multiset does
carry signal (`maxDegree = 1` with `w = n` says all-linear), a worst-case gate
just cannot use it.

## Blockers

None. Two protocol lessons worth keeping. A single-pass corpus sweep is not a
before/after instrument on a shared host: it put the long Swinnerton-Dyer rows at
1.25x to 1.31x where the counterbalanced paired driver puts them inside its
control band. And the durable sweep record needs the tree clean for the whole
run, because the freshness check rejects a record whose `git_dirty` is true --
editing a report while a sweep runs invalidates it.
