# Prime-plan pricing (issue #9156)

## Accomplished

Replaced the direct prime planner's fixed `scoutWidth = 8` stop threshold with
`Hex.scoutPays`, one deterministic comparison between the recombination work the
plan in hand still has to do and the scouts plus split that replacing it would
cost. Both sides are estimated from shape already observed -- input degree,
primes, degree multisets -- and share a factor `n^2` which cancels, so the rule
is one inequality with two calibrated integer ratios.

- `HexBerlekampZassenhaus/Modular/PrimePlan.lean`: `liftWords`,
  `scoutRoundCost`, `splitColumnCost`, `ScoutIncumbent`, `scoutPays`.
  `scoutBetterPattern` now carries the incumbent plan rather than a bare width,
  and `firstDirectPlan?` lost its width branch. Both `DirectPrimePlan` facts
  stay proved and their proofs got shorter by one case each.
- `scripts/bench/prime_policy_replay.py`: offline replay of eight policies over
  recorded per-candidate costs, plus the decision-margin table and a check that
  the replayed pre-change policy reproduces the recorded binary's selection.
- `scripts/bench/prime_plan_paired.py`: names the measured core to its child
  (`--cpu auto` in the child was resetting the inherited affinity, so the two
  arms could land on different cores), and takes `--changed` because a dropped
  scout moves neither the selected prime nor the split count.
- `bench/HexBench/FactorService.lean`: the counterfactual's fixed comparison set
  is pinned with its own historical constants; scout rows carry `liftWords`,
  `recombUnits` and `splitMaxDegree`.
- Conformance guards pin both directions of `scoutPays` and the modulus width.
- `reports/hexbz-prime-plan-pricing.md`, and the SPEC's prime-selection section.

The two constants come from measured medians, but two records of the same source
disagree about them by tens of percent (10.2 and 5.2 on one, 13.4 and 4.4 on
another), so do not read the shipped 10 and 5 as precise. What matters is that
120 of 120 ratio pairs over 6--20 and 2--9 give the identical decision on every
instance; the replay's `--sensitivity` flag checks that.

## Current frontier

Six corpus instances change their walk, and all six improve: paired,
`cyclo_phi105_x_phi128` 0.410x, `cyclo_phi64_x_phi105` 0.407x, `sd4_x_phi17`
0.440x, `legendre_P18` 0.603x, `cyclo_phi24_x_phi35` 0.752x, `cyclo_phi275`
0.791x, against a load control of 0.989x to 1.049x. Only
`cyclo_phi64_x_phi105` is in the issue #9127 representative set, so the
representative-set run alone understates the change; the other five were found by
running both binaries over the whole corpus.

On all six the decision is confirmed by the attainable saving -- the incumbent's
measured downstream less the cheapest downstream any other priced candidate
offers -- against the observation's measured cost. Three of them have no better
prime in the comparison set at all.

Two protocol lessons worth keeping. Comparing against the newest committed sweep
record is not a control when another branch produced it; run both binaries. And
the confirmation check has to be attainable saving, not the incumbent's
recombination against the observation cost -- the latter flattered the rule on
rows where no better prime existed.

## Next step

Nine first-decisions on the representative set are unconfirmed, and every one is
an *acceptance* the fixed threshold also made -- the equal-width families, where
`2^(w-1)` says 2e12 candidates remain and 40 are visited. So the change removes
six observations that could not pay and introduces none. No function of the
degree multiset can separate a Wilkinson image (all linear, all rational roots,
search ends at level one) from `sd5` (sixteen quadratics, irreducible, search
exhausts): whether the search ends early is a fact about the answer. Tightening
that needs evidence about reducibility, not a better cost model.

Also unconfirmable from these records: nine narrow declines have no alternative
downstream, because the counterfactual prices the fixed rule's retained set,
which stops at the first good prime when it is narrow. Widening it to the whole
scouting horizon would price them, at the cost of replaying downstreams no policy
under test selects (36 s on `xpow105_minus1` alone).

Per the issue's last dependency note, `powModMonicAux` work belongs in a
separate kernel issue opened from the post-change profile.

## Blockers

None. The measurement protocol needs the tree clean while the sweep runs: the
freshness check rejects a record whose `git_dirty` is true, and an editing
session that writes a report during a sweep invalidates it.
