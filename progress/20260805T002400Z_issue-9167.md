# Multiquadratic tower degree from independent square classes (#9167)

## Accomplished

`HexBerlekampZassenhausMathlib/SquareClass.lean` (new, wired into
`HexBerlekampZassenhausMathlib.All`) carries part (1) of the tower theorem from
`reports/hexbz-quadratic-norm-certificate.md`:

- `Hex.SquareClass.Independent ds`: no nonempty `List.Sublist` of `ds` has a
  product that is a square in `ℚ`. `independent_iff_sublists` restates it over
  `ℤ` and over `ds.sublists`, which is the shape an executable Bool check
  decides. `Independent.of_perm` and `Independent.sublist` give the symmetry and
  monotonicity the later parts need.
- `isSquare_or_isSquare_mul` (Lemma A) over an arbitrary field `F` of
  characteristic `≠ 2` with `r ^ 2 = a` in an extension `E`. The `{1, r}`
  representation it needs is `exists_repr_of_mem_adjoin`, proved by reducing a
  polynomial expression modulo `X ^ 2 - C a` rather than by building a basis.
- `adjoinSqrt ds : IntermediateField ℚ ℂ` (adjoin every complex square root of
  every entry), `adjoinSqrt_cons_sup`, `adjoinSqrt_of_perm`.
- `not_isSquareIn_prod`: the strengthened claim, over a `built ++ rest` split of
  the radicands rather than an index cut. `not_isSquareIn_of_perm_cons` exposes
  it at an arbitrary index.
- `finrank_adjoinSqrt : Module.finrank ℚ (adjoinSqrt ds) = 2 ^ ds.length`.

- `decidableIndependent`, plus `sqrt_mem_adjoinSqrt` / `sqrt_notMem_adjoinSqrt`
  for the primitive-element step and `adjoinSqrt_mono` for the containment step.

Zero `sorry`/`axiom`/`native_decide`; `#print axioms` on the headline theorems
reports only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

## Notes for the next agent

`Independent` is decidable (`decidableIndependent`, via
`independent_iff_sublists` and Mathlib's `DecidablePred (IsSquare : ℤ → Prop)`),
but plain `decide` sticks: the integer perfect-square test runs `Nat.sqrt`, whose
well-founded recursion the elaborator's `whnf` will not unfold. `decide +kernel`
does reduce it. The executable Bool check still owes the `Array Int` bridge and
the bitmask-enumeration correspondence.


`IntermediateField.restrictScalars` picks a different `Algebra ℚ ↥K` instance
path depending on whether the term is written by hand or produced by unifying a
Mathlib lemma's statement, so `rw [restrictScalars_adjoin_eq_sup]` and
`rw [extendScalars_restrictScalars]` both report "did not find pattern" on goals
that print identically to the pattern. Apply those lemmas as terms
(`exact`/`Eq.trans`/`SetLike.ext_iff`), which unifies up to defeq. This file
states its tower step over `⊔` for that reason and only crosses to
`restrictScalars` inside proofs.
