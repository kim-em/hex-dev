# Characteristic polynomial SPEC

## Accomplished

- Wrote `SPEC/Libraries/hex-char-poly.md` covering `hex-char-poly` and
  `hex-char-poly-mathlib`: the Samuelson-Berkowitz recursion stated by
  trailing block size with a worked `n = 2` check, the executable API,
  the coefficient/degree/monicity theorems, the Mathlib correspondence,
  the Cayley-Hamilton boundary, complexity with a proved operand-growth
  bound, conformance, benchmarking, file organisation, and open
  questions.
- Checked every proposed signature against the tree: `Region`,
  `mulVec`, `dotProduct`, `Vector.reverse/ofFn`, `DensePoly.ofCoeffs`,
  `coeff`, `degree?`, `Monic`, `leadingCoeff_zero`, `One (DensePoly R)`,
  `HexMatrixMathlib.matrixEquiv`, `HexPolyMathlib.equiv`,
  `HexMatrixMathlib.det_eq`, and the Mathlib `Matrix.charpoly` family.
- Recorded two gaps found while checking: hex-matrix has no `trace`
  anywhere, and hex-poly proves the `DensePoly` ring laws without
  assembling a `Lean.Grind.CommRing (DensePoly R)` instance, which is
  what blocks a Mathlib-free Cayley-Hamilton. A scalar-matrix
  constructor is *not* a gap: `t • Hex.Matrix.identity n` works through
  the existing `SMul` instance.
- Corrected three real errors after a Codex review, each verified
  independently first. The operand-growth section claimed intermediates
  stay within a constant factor of the output, which cancellation
  refutes (`[[H, H], [-H, -H]]` gives `x²` while Berkowitz forms `H²`).
  Transpose invariance does have a recursion-level argument, so its
  companion placement is proof engineering rather than reach. And
  `dotProduct` is `Vector.dotProduct`, not `Hex.Matrix.dotProduct`.
  Also replaced "Laplace expansion" in the Mathlib proof plan with the
  bordered-determinant identity that actually produces the moments.
- Updated `SPEC/Libraries/README.md` (summary lists, dependency lists,
  DAG section, index) and replaced the now-superseded sketch in
  `SPEC/future-work.md` with a pointer, following the
  Hermite/Smith graduation precedent in that file.
