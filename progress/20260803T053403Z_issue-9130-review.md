## Current frontier

No soundness defect was found: the dependent target index is substantive, and
the production obstruction and exact division use the same target. The review
findings are in measurement methodology: the obstruction-probe rows are not
committed as a raw artifact, the `sameDivisors` check compares counts and degree
lists rather than divisor polynomials, the paired probe order is fixed, and the
near-neutral overhead claims are underpowered on a shared host. The large
`xpow` speedup remains well separated from the observed noise.
