# 2026-08-04 — issue #9157: root extraction for completely split modular images

## Accomplished

**Measurement gate.** Added `bench/HexBench/RootSplitProbe.lean`
(`hexbz_root_split_probe`), which prices the residue scan, the linear-factor
construction, and the `X^p mod f` certificate against `berlekampFactor` across
Wilkinson rows at their production-selected primes, synthetic completely split
inputs over a degree/field-size grid, wide-field controls, mixed-degree
controls, and controls sitting just inside the candidate budget. Record:
`reports/bench-results/hexbz-root-split-probe-f87a0c7d.json`; analysis:
`reports/berlekamp-root-extraction-measurement.md`.

Two findings decided the design:

* The `X^p mod f` certificate is *not* cheaper than the scan it would guard
  (295 µs against 237 µs at `d = 64`, `p = 67`) and is recomputed inside the
  fixed-space matrix afterwards, so it was rejected. The scan is its own
  certificate: the result is used only when the scan finds `deg f` roots, and
  that length test is what the reconstruction proof consumes.
* A budget of `25 · p ≤ (deg f)^2` — the point where the scan costs about a
  twenty-fifth of the matrix build it might replace — captures every Wilkinson
  row while holding an admitted-but-rejected scan to 0.8–2.4% of
  `berlekampFactor`.

**Implementation.** `HexBerlekamp/LinearFactors.lean` consolidates the monic
linear-factor lemmas that the Rabin `X^p - X` product identity and the new path
share (they were private in `RabinSoundness/RabinShape.lean`), adds root
enumeration `rootsIn`, and proves `eq_foldl_rootsIn_of_length`: a monic `f`
whose scan finds `deg f` roots is the product of the corresponding monic linear
factors. `Hex.Berlekamp.berlekampFactor` now has one selection point and two
branches; every public theorem about its output is proved for both.
