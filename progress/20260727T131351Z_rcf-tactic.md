# RCF proof-producing tactic

## Accomplished

- Added kernel transport lemmas for all six comparisons under subtraction and
  positive denominator scaling, plus real and dyadic-interval quantifier
  transport.
- Added one-variable real polynomial reification for exact rational numerals,
  addition, subtraction, multiplication, negation, natural powers, and division
  by closed nonzero rational scalars. Each atom carries a kernel-checked ring
  identity after clearing one positive common denominator.
- Added reification for `True`, `False`, negation, conjunction, disjunction,
  implication, all six comparisons, one real universal or existential, and the
  corresponding `Set.Ioc` forms with exactly represented dyadic endpoints.
- Added literal quotation for the full `Sentence` and `Certificate` trees,
  including generalized Sturm replays, carrier/common-root witnesses,
  isolations, sign matrices, and size-indexed endpoint vectors.
- Added the `rcf` tactic. Compiled search only selects evidence; generated proofs
  close goals through literal `Certificate.check`, `check_sound`, and the
  reifier's kernel-checked equivalence. False verdicts never produce proofs.
- Added exact false-universal cell diagnostics (dyadic samples or root
  intervals), existential exhaustion diagnostics, unsupported-fragment
  messages, and rewrite guidance for `Set.Icc` and `Set.Ioo`.
- Added tactic regressions for all comparisons and Boolean forms, rational
  denominator clearing, all four quantifiers, half-open endpoint ownership,
  constant/no-root/root cases, equal and reversed intervals, sibling-goal
  preservation, false verdicts, and every required fall-through class.
- Wired the reifier, tactic, and tests into the umbrella. Focused builds, the
  full 9484-job monorepo build, structural trust checks, and diff checks pass.
- Merged the preceding decision-assembly PR after hosted CI passed. Two
  independent Sol reviews found no soundness defect; their one tactic-state
  composability finding was fixed and pinned with a multi-goal regression.
