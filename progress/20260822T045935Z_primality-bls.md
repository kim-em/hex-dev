# hex-primality: the BLS m = 1 theorem (milestone 4a)

## Accomplished

- `HexPrimality/Cert3.lean`: `pocklington3`, the cube-root
  Brillhart-Lehmer-Selfridge criterion at `m = 1`, proved Mathlib-free
  with no sorries. The argument: a composite `n` splits as
  `q · (n / q)` with both divisors `≡ 1 (mod F)` (`divisor_mod_one`
  by strong induction through `exists_prime_dvd`), giving
  `n = (Fa + 1)(Fb + 1)` and `abF + (a + b) = 2sF + r`; parity forces
  the mod-`F` offset `k` even (`R` odd + `F` even make `a + b` odd,
  hence `ab` even, hence `k ≡ 0 (mod 2)`); `k ≥ 2` on one side breaks
  `r < 2F` and on the other collides with `ab ≥ a + b - 1` plus the
  size-bound cap `2s ≤ 2F + r` (`two_s_le`); so `a + b = r`,
  `ab = 2s`, and `r² = (a - b)² + 8s` finishes against the
  discriminant condition.
- Also `not_square_of_sqrt_witness`, the two-multiplication witness
  form of the integer-square test the checker will use in place of
  the non-kernel-reducible `Nat.sqrt` (the SPEC amendment).
- Every nonlinear step is an explicit expansion lemma
  (`sq_expand`, `discriminant_eq`, `add_le_mul_succ`, `two_s_le`)
  with AC-normalized product atoms handed to omega.
