# Issue #9160 measurement review

## Accomplished

Reviewed PR #9165's attribution ladder, stage mirror, doubled-multiply probe,
raw merged benchmark record, generated C, and report conclusions against the
production packed kernel. No implementation or report changes were made.

The source-row hoist has a large, credible signal on the arithmetic-heavy rows,
and the current generated C contains both intended remainder calls. The review
also found that the flat rung changes the outer elimination loop as well as the
row write, the multiply delta includes `min` and is not a lower bound on the
cost of removing the shipped multiply, and the Barrett conclusion weights tiny
and rank-zero rows equally even though Barrett wins slightly on all four
largest arithmetic-heavy reductions.

## Current frontier

The measurement is useful evidence for pursuing the pivot-row hoist, but the
one-factor ladder and several report conclusions are not yet methodologically
sound. In particular, the existing report's numbered candidate causes do not
match the causes named by the new attribution report, and the three raw repeat
samples are discarded after independent per-field medians.

## Next step

Split the flat rung into matched controls for the inner row-write and outer
column-scan changes; measure multiply/min overhead in the same production or
hoisted loop shape; retain paired raw samples with counterbalanced rung order;
and revise the Barrett, readback, and exact source-copy claims to match what the
data isolate.
