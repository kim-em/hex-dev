## Two preregistration defects that made a release verdict unreachable

Neither was visible until a run was actually attempted.

1. **Null controls too close.** The harness needs its two same-module
   controls to differ in build magnitude by >= 2.0x. Every probe carries the
   same ~6.65 s `import all` closure, so `Irreducible16` is only 1.37x the
   baseline. A run could finish with every arm clean and still be rejected
   with "null-control build magnitudes are not sufficiently distinct".
   `Kernel8` is 2.45x and is also the costliest substantive arm, so it is
   both admissible and the control that makes every envelope interpolated
   rather than extrapolated.

2. **Retry budget too small for these arm lengths.** Arms run 6.61–16.32 s,
   ~3x the sibling suite's, so `ratio x wall` binds instead of the 0.030 s
   quantization floor and each arm is proportionally longer exposed to a
   stray scheduler tick. On a busy shared host the default eight retries
   exhaust on the longest arms.

## The mistake worth recording

For (2) I first raised `--max-core-interference-ratio` from 0.005 to 0.015
and argued it merely reproduced the sibling's *effective* admitted bound.
Codex review demolished this, correctly:

- the arithmetic in my own report was wrong (I quoted the low end at 0.005
  and the high end at 0.015);
- the sibling's 0.014862 is its *shortest, quantization-limited* arm; across
  its range the effective ceiling runs 0.0147 down to 0.0069, so quoting the
  max cherry-picked the friendliest arm;
- 0.015 > 0.014862 is looser, not tighter;
- the run admitted 0.224 s into one arm against the sibling's 0.030 s.

The right lever was in `SPEC/benchmarking.md` all along: a suite with
preregistered long arms may request up to 32 retries, and the retry bound
"changes only how many clean-pair opportunities are attempted, never the
admission threshold". I had considered and dismissed it early on the false
belief that these arms were ~4–6 s; I had already measured 6.6–16.6 s by
then. **Check whether a knob moves the threshold or only the number of
attempts before reaching for it.**

Re-run at 0.005 with 32 retries: complete, release-quality, max admitted
aggregate ratio 0.004743, 122 rejected attempts and 126 rejected preflight
windows for 66 clean pairs.

The stricter gate produced *better* evidence, not just cleaner bookkeeping:
the baseline control's own spread fell from 0.2170 s to 0.0385 s, so
`factor-4` and `multiplicity-8` went from unresolved to resolved. All nine
substantive arms now resolve.
