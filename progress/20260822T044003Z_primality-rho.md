# hex-primality: Brent rho and the partial factorization (milestone 3b)

## Accomplished

- `HexPrimality/Search.lean` (part 1): `RhoStop`/`RhoFailure` (the
  resumable-failure convention, advanced `Rand` carried on error),
  `rhoFactor?` as Brent cycle search with restart draws from
  `Hex.Rand` and **dynamic validation** at every success exit, so
  `rhoFactor?_spec` (`1 < d ∧ d < n ∧ d ∣ n`) is proved by fuel
  induction with the guards carrying the whole obligation. Public,
  since hex-int-factor reuses exactly this primitive.
- The internal `partialFactor`: trial division over the committed
  table (`trialGo`/`divOut`), then a rho worklist (`rhoPhase`) that
  keeps probable primes, splits what rho cracks, and multiplies
  everything else into the residual; fuel exhaustion dumps the stack
  into the residual so the product is exact in every path.
  `partialFactor_prod` is the unconditional product invariant.
- `#guard`s: every `rhoFactor?` result shape (invalid, even, split
  semiprime, prime exhaustion) and the product invariant at runtime.
