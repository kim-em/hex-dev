## Accomplished

Wrote `SPEC/Libraries/hex-mv-factor.md`, the multivariate factorization
SPEC for `MvPoly n Int cmp` and its Mathlib companion, and registered it
in `SPEC/Libraries/README.md` (summary lists, dependency lists, DAG
paragraph and diagram, index) and in the `SPEC/future-work.md` entry it
expands.

Design decisions worth restating, since they are the ones a reader is
most likely to want to challenge:

- Two claims, two types. `Decomp` with `checkDecomp` carries the product
  identity; `Complete` with `checkIrred` carries irreducibility. A
  failed search is never an input to either.
- `checkIrred` is a *conditional* checker: it reduces multivariate
  irreducibility to a named list of univariate irreducibility
  obligations, which the companion discharges through
  hex-berlekamp-zassenhaus-mathlib. This is design principle 2 applied
  to an irreducibility hypothesis rather than to analysis.
- Four certificate constructors: `degreeOne`, `image`, `embed`, and the
  unconditional `kronecker` route (variables packed into one variable,
  univariate factorization, subset sweep with exact division). The last
  needs no coefficient bound, which is what makes irreducibility
  decidable in the companion.
- A failed lift is deliberately *not* a refutation constructor. The
  hypotheses of `no_lift_of_reconstruct` are `valid`, `BoundsFactors`,
  and `2 * B < modulus`, and the modulus schedule does not reach that
  bound; the SPEC says so instead of quietly using the weaker fact.

## Findings against the accepted hex-mv-hensel SPEC

- `irreducible_of_image_irreducible` does **not** apply to the images
  this library builds. Wang's leading-coefficient correction rescales
  each univariate factor by an integer `γ_j = L_j(a) / lc h_j`, and that
  theorem's hypothesis is `Irred (inp.images[j])`, which is false when
  `γ_j` is a nonunit. hex-mv-factor proves the scaled form itself (one
  extra Gauss step at arity one); an amendment to hex-mv-hensel is
  recorded under "Open questions".
- The same rescaling is what makes V3 an identity over `ℤ`:
  `∏_j γ_j = scalar`, so `∏_j F_j = imageAt a f` exactly, while V4's
  value condition holds by construction. hex-mv-hensel only says
  "folding that scalar into one of the `F_j` is the caller's job"; doing
  it that way satisfies V3 and breaks V4.
- V1 through V6 each have a named source, tabulated in the SPEC's
  step 5. V6 is the only one that does not survive a coarser
  recombination grouping, so `witnessOf?` is rerun there.
