# issue-9152: separate lift support from residual target images

## Accomplished

- Replaced `Hex.SupportMeta basis target` with `Hex.LiftSupport basis` (prepared
  lift modulus, per-factor degrees and trailing coefficients, and their proof
  fields) plus the existing `Hex.TargetImage target`. No compatibility wrapper
  remains; the mixed structure and `Hex.supportMeta` are gone.
- `Hex.peelDirect` prepares the lift support once per peel run.
  `Hex.peelDirectAux` reduces each residual once and hands that reduction to
  `Hex.findDirectSubset`, which reuses it across the residual's whole
  cardinality schedule. An exact split retains the lift support and builds a
  fresh image for the quotient, so a `TargetImage` never outlives the
  polynomial its type names.
- The head-forced traversal takes the same two objects rather than the mixed
  one. `Hex.scanDirectLevel` still prepares them per level, which is where the
  mixed object left them; hoisting them ripples into the classical completeness
  and correctness proofs and buys one preparation per level, amortized over that
  level's leaves. Recorded as a successor, not done here.
- Added `scripts/bench/proposal_construction_counts.py`, which derives the exact
  per-row construction counts from `--entry proposalTrace`.
- Wrote `reports/hexbz-lift-support-lifetimes.md` with the counts, the
  trace-equality check, the per-row paired timings, the paired corpus sweep and
  the same-arm noise floor.

## Findings worth carrying forward

- **Only 23 of the 392 corpus rows enter proposal search at all.**
  `Hex.proposalEligible` needs at least 24 lifted modular factors and
  `degree < 4 * nonzeroCoefficientCount`. The rows #9152 nominates for
  measurement (`sd5_x_phi11`, `sd4_x_sd4shift1`, `xpow48/105/120`) are *not*
  proposal-eligible: their completed cardinality levels belong to the
  head-forced classical engine, not to the traversal this issue changes. The
  rows that do exercise it are the Swinnerton-Dyer sixes and sevens, the van
  Hoeij instances, and Wilkinson 24 and up. Any future proposal-search issue
  should draw its row list from that set.
- The construction counts are exactly derivable from the recorded proposal
  trace, with no new production counters: attempted levels are
  `len(unforcedCompletedLevels) + peels`, and residuals searched are
  `peels + 1` when the run stopped inside `findDirectSubset`. That keeps
  `ClassicalStats` unchanged while still measuring the thing the issue asks for.
- Wilkinson is where the lift-preparation saving concentrates (228 of 269
  preparations) and where the target-image saving is provably zero, because
  every Wilkinson residual succeeds at cardinality one. Both halves of that
  prediction in the issue hold.

## Current frontier

Measurements: counts move 269 to 23 lift preparations and 269 to 247 target
reductions over the 23 affected rows; all 392 traces are identical on both arms;
every timing figure is inside the measured noise floor, which is the outcome
#9152 pre-registered as acceptable.
