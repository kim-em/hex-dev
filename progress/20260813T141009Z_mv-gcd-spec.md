# Multivariate gcd and squarefree decomposition SPEC

## Accomplished

Wrote `SPEC/Libraries/hex-mv-gcd.md`, expanding the "Multivariate gcd and
squarefree decomposition" entry in `SPEC/future-work.md` into a full
library specification for `hex-mv-gcd` and `hex-mv-gcd-mathlib`, then
revised it after an adversarial review.

### What the SPEC settles

The architecture is one verified checker plus five unverified candidate
producers (structural reductions, coprime detection, a Kronecker
heuristic, Brown, Zippel), with a subresultant fallback whose success is
the theorem to prove. Certificate: cofactors plus a recursive
coprimality witness carrying an active-variable set, with a modular
constructor (prime, coefficient homomorphism, point in the target field,
Bézout pair over `FpPoly p`) and a `splitBezout` alternative.

Three findings changed the design away from what `future-work.md`
assumed:

1. **The Bézout coprimality witness does not exist.** `ℤ[x]` is not a
   Bézout domain (`x` and `2` are coprime with no identity), and neither
   is `R[x₁, …, xₙ]` for `n ≥ 2` even over a field.

2. **The arity-dropping recursive view costs more than it saves here**,
   so the SPEC adds an arity-preserving `coeffsIn`/`ofCoeffsIn` over a
   `Without i R cmp` subtype, recorded as an addition needed in
   hex-mv-poly along with a linear `mapCoeffs` and a Mathlib-free
   `Lean.Grind.CommRing (MvPoly n R cmp)`.

3. **hex-resultant needs the Grind ring tower to apply its theorems.**
   The executable chain runs over `MvPoly` once `Div` and `ExactDivLaws`
   exist; its correctness theorems all take `[Lean.Grind.CommRing S]`,
   and the `DensePoly` tower is built in
   `HexResultant/ExactDiv.lean:255-434`. No `MvPoly` counterpart is
   specified anywhere.

### What the review changed

A Codex review (30 findings) plus a self-audit found the first draft was
not implementation-ready. The substantive corrections now in the file:

- **Circularity.** `checkCoprime_sound`'s content step needs
  `dvd_contentIn`, which is gcd maximality one variable down, and the
  draft deferred maximality to the companion while claiming an
  unconditional Mathlib-free soundness theorem. Resolved by a
  `LawfulContent` class: the Mathlib-free theorem is conditional, and
  `hex-mv-gcd-mathlib` discharges it from
  `MvPolynomial.uniqueFactorizationMonoid`. Consequence recorded: the
  unconditional statement, and therefore `cancel`, lives in the
  companion.
- **Squarefree over `ℤ`.** `gcd(2x, 2) = 2` made the draft's
  `isSquarefree` return false for a squarefree input and its `radical`
  drop the factor `2`. The test now runs on the primitive part, the
  decomposition's scalar is `content : R` (not a unit: `p = 6` gives
  `⟨6, []⟩`), and `Squarefree` means every repeated divisor is constant.
  The ring-theoretic predicate over `ℤ` needs integer factorization and
  is deferred to that item.
- **The decision procedure now uses all `n` partial derivatives** and is
  correct in characteristic zero *and* perfect positive characteristic,
  in both directions. `radical` is characteristic-zero only (`x³` in
  characteristic 3 returns `1`).
- **Positive-characteristic decomposition was wrong** and is now scoped
  rather than sketched. `x^p + y` has `∂/∂x = 0`, is squarefree, and is
  not a `p`-th power; `(x^p+y)²·(x+y^p)²` shows no single main variable
  works. The obstruction is that `F_q[x₂ … xₙ]` is not perfect, so the
  univariate fix does not apply level by level. Gianni-Trager (AAECC 7,
  1996) is named as the algorithm to follow.
- **`gcd_reorder_int` was false.** Negation flips every sign, but which
  coefficient is leading changes with the order: `x - y` normalises to
  `x - y` under one order and `y - x` under another. Only the
  `Associated` statement survives.
- **Route 5 mixed two algorithms.** Taking primitive parts inside the
  subresultant recurrence destroys its scale invariants. The SPEC now
  runs hex-resultant's chain unchanged and takes the primitive part once
  at the end.
- **`subresultantChainExt` is not an export.** `subresultantAux` keeps
  only `(pseudoDivMod prev curr).2` (`HexResultant/Subresultant.lean:65`,
  `:92`); the quotient is discarded, so cofactor tracking is a new
  recurrence.
- **`coeffsIn_ofCoeffsIn` was false** without the `Without` subtype, and
  `divExact?_eq` was false at `g = 0`.
- **GCDHEU needs a mixed-radix substitution** (successive powers collide
  `x₂` with `x₁²`) and a bit-size budget, and its reconstruction bound
  proves nothing about the answer.
- **Brown was missing** `γ(a) = 0` rejection, the whole prime-level
  bad/unlucky/support-stabilisation layer, and its "smallest main
  variable minimises images" claim was wrong.
- **Yun runs one gcd per level**, not per distinct multiplicity.
- **Conformance could not catch the bugs it claimed to.** Because every
  route falls through to route 5, oracle fixtures pass with Brown
  entirely broken. The suite is now split into route-level tests that
  assert on internals and oracle fixtures that check the answer.
- Smaller: `LawfulGcdOps` gained domain, normalisation, and `Dvd` laws;
  `normUnit 0 = 1`; the modular certificate carries an explicit
  `CoeffHom` and its point lives in the target field; the nesting claim
  was removed (`MvPoly` is not Bézout); "trial division is the decision"
  was corrected to "`checkGcd` is the decision" throughout; the
  complexity table is relabelled as probe counts; the FLINT comparison
  is scoped to the families where routes correspond, since no sparse
  Hensel route is specified.

Smaller edits elsewhere: added `hex-mv-poly` and `hex-mv-gcd` to the
index in `SPEC/Libraries/README.md` (hex-mv-poly was missing), and
cross-reference pointers from both `future-work.md` entries, with the
Bézout correction, the content-maximality dependency, and the `ℤ`
squarefree caveat stated there.

## Current frontier

The SPEC is written and checked against `HexMvPoly` as it stands on
`main`, where the library is finished (`done_through: 7`, `status:
active`) and its SPEC lives at `HexMvPoly/SPEC/hex-mv-poly.md`. The three
additions it asks for were verified absent from the source, not inferred
from the SPEC text, and one earlier claim did not survive that check:
`Structural.bind` already maps the coefficient type, so what is missing
is a linear `mapCoeffs` (the `bind` fold costs one polynomial addition
per term) and the homomorphism laws, not the map itself. `monoContent`
came off the list too, since `Mono.gcd` exists and the fold belongs here.

`HexMvGcd` is not in `libraries.yml`; the SPEC records the entry it would
gain, at `status: draft`.

Two mismatches between `HexMvPoly`'s SPEC text and its source are noted
in the new file: the constructors take `[BEq R] [LawfulBEq R]` rather
than `[DecidableEq R]`, and evaluation and substitution take
`[Lean.Grind.Semiring R]`.

The index in `SPEC/Libraries/README.md` had a dangling link to
`hex-mv-poly.md` (the file moved to the library directory when
hex-mv-poly graduated) and still described hex-mv-poly-mathlib as
planned. Both are fixed here.

## Next step

Land the three additions in `HexMvPoly` before milestone 1: `coeffsIn` /
`ofCoeffsIn` / `leadingCoeffIn` / `degreeIn?` over the `Without` subtype
in `HexMvPoly/Recursive.lean`, a linear `mapCoeffs` with homomorphism
laws, and a Mathlib-free `Lean.Grind.CommRing (MvPoly n R cmp)` tower
following `HexResultant/ExactDiv.lean:255-434`. The last is what makes
hex-resultant's correctness theorems apply over multivariate
coefficients, not just its executable chain.

The `subresultantChainExt` addition to hex-resultant is not on the
required path and should wait for the certificate benchmark question.

## Blockers

None for the SPEC. Two things are deferred with their reasons written
down rather than solved: positive-characteristic squarefree
decomposition (needs Gianni-Trager, milestone 5), and
`Decidable (Squarefree p)` over `ℤ` (needs the integer factorization
item).
