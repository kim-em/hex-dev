# Lean v4.34.0-rc2 toolchain update

## Accomplished

- Updated the Lean toolchain, Mathlib, and Verso pins together from
  `v4.33.0-rc1` to `v4.34.0-rc2`, then regenerated `lake-manifest.json` with
  `lake update`.
- Adapted six Mathlib-facing files for the new elaboration and simplification
  behavior: removed obsolete named parameters and now-redundant tactic calls,
  made the rational-cast power proof explicit, and gave one expensive theorem
  a narrowly scoped 400,000-heartbeat allowance.
- Confirmed that the complete `lake build` succeeds (9,962 jobs after rebasing
  onto the latest `origin/main`), that Mathlib is fully cached via
  `lake build --no-build Mathlib`,
  and that the workflow's structural/source lint and unit-test preflight passes.
- Confirmed that the factorization performance corpus is current. The local
  PR run then correctly rejected the old Hex measurement because toolchain
  pins are runtime-relevant under `SPEC/CI.md`.
- Rebuilt `hexbz_factor_service`, measured the 392-case `hex-factor` corpus on
  an automatically selected idle CPU, and committed the cross-checked result:
  383 cases solved in 109.8 seconds with no status changes versus the prior
  v4.33 record. Across the 383 commonly solved cases, the median and geometric
  mean runtime ratios were 1.0057 and 1.0061 respectively (new/old).
- Regenerated all 25 factorization figures with the CI-pinned Matplotlib 3.11.1
  and confirmed both freshness checks pass locally.
