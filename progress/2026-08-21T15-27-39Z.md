# Supported frontend semantic model

## Accomplished

- Made the recursive frontend retain and authenticate its exact target term and
  every term/node/operation/ordered-input correspondence.
- Derived the complete real `Program.Models` interpretation from caller source
  values, with a bounded fail-closed builder and exact root-evaluation proof.
- Added decoded-result depth preflight before recursive term validation.
- Added model-derived lower, upper, conjunction, and singleton-equality closure
  APIs plus discriminating conformance for malformed roots, operation tables,
  and resource limits.
- Updated the current-state specifications to distinguish this supported
  programmatic bridge from the still-missing Lean-expression and search-recipe
  frontends.

## Current frontier

- The supported flat frontend can reify a `Term`, derive its semantics, replay
  a caller chronology, and close an ordinary theorem without a caller-provided
  `Program.Models` proof.

## Next step
