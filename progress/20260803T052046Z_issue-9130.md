# 2026-08-03 — issue #9130, word-prime divisibility obstruction

## Accomplished

Recombination candidates are now put to a finite-field necessary condition
before exact integer division. Reduction modulo a fixed word-sized prime is a
ring homomorphism `ℤ[X] → 𝔽_q[X]`, so a nonzero `𝔽_q[X]` remainder proves the
candidate does not divide.

- `HexBerlekampZassenhaus/Classical/Obstruction.lean`: `Hex.obstructionPrime`
  (`2^26 - 5`, primality by kernel-checked trial division), `Hex.TargetImage`,
  `Hex.obstructs`, and the theorems — `obstructs_eq` (optimized computation
  equals the reference remainder), `obstructs_eq_false_of_dvd` (no false
  rejection), `not_dvd_of_obstructs`, `exactQuotient?_eq_none_of_obstructs`.
- `Hex.directCandidateAfterObstruction` plus
  `directCandidateAfterObstruction_eq`, which is why `Hex.directLeaf_eq` and
  every classical completeness proof above it are untouched.
- `Hex.SupportMeta` is now indexed by the target and carries its reduced
  image, so the reduction happens once per subset-cardinality level rather
  than once per candidate. Both the head-forced traversal and the unforced
  proposal sweep consume it.
- Benchmark-only prototype first: `hexbz_factor_service --entry
  obstructionProbe` runs the counted mirror twice on one input, with and
  without the filter, and reports both stage-counter sets and both spans. It
  measured the filter before production consumed it, and it is still the
  before/after instrument.
- `reports/hexbz-modular-obstruction.md` with a fresh full sweep, phase
  profile, and sampling profile at this revision *and* at the branch's merge
  base, all committed.

Measured: 4,204 of the 4,302 candidates reaching the filter were rejected, and
all 98 that passed were genuine divisors. Aggregate `xpow48/105/120` 0.378x,
whole corpus 0.699x, `cyclo_phi1031` from timeout to 6.470 s. Median over rows
above one millisecond 0.999x; cost side (rows where nearly every candidate
divides) 1.016x.

## Current frontier

`sd5` moves only 0.942x. Its 32,768 leaves are rejected by the metadata-only
prefilters long before the obstruction sees them, so the obstruction removes
128 of 129 exact divisions and that is worth 5.8% — exact division was never
`sd5`'s cost.

## Next step

The residual `sd5` cost is `Hex.centeredModNat` at 10.3% of total, and the
mechanism is narrow: `directTrailingPrefilter` passes the modulus as a `Nat`,
so every leaf evaluates `Int.ofNat m` on a 93-bit value and allocates a fresh
`mpz`, while `Hex.SupportMeta` already carries `modulusInt`. Threading the
integer modulus through would remove the conversion. It changes
`directCandidatePrefilter`'s signature, which the classical completeness proofs
consume, so it wants its own issue rather than being bundled here.

## Blockers

None. The two premises in #9130's body that did not survive contact are
recorded in the report: the modular image cannot be assembled from cached
per-factor reductions (centred lifting does not commute with reduction modulo
`q`), and there is no inconclusive branch to write, because
`DivModLaws.mod_eq_zero_of_dvd` covers a zero or degree-dropped divisor with no
side condition.
